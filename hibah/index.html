Your funding position

Digunakan untuk menganggar jangka masa perancangan dan menyemak pakej yang berkaitan dengan anda.

Money that stays yours and keeps earning — it is only used at your death to pay the court costs.

Only certain instruments can be formally earmarked for this purpose. Pick the one you would use — the expected return decides whether the fund keeps pace with rising legal costs.

These four are the sources named in the WSB price list: Tabung Haji, Unit Amanah, Kasihku (ASNB) and Takaful. If your money currently sits somewhere else — a fixed deposit, EPF, shares, ETFs, gold, property rental — it cannot be earmarked where it is, because the peruntukan requires a Pra Hibah document over that specific account. It would need to be moved into one of the four first, so choose the option matching where it would go, not where it sits today. Other instruments may be considered on confirmation with WSB.
