Class Notes · Siri 19 · Part 1

Surah Al-Humazah

Thursday, 19 November 2026  |  Surah ke-104 · 9 ayat · Makkiyyah  |  Al-Humazah = pengumpat / pencela
In short
  • Kecelakaan bagi orang yang suka mengumpat & mencela — and for the one who hoards and keeps counting his harta.
  • Both come from the same root: rasa diri lebih baik & serba cukup (Al-'Alaq 96:6–7).
  • Sombong isn't about dressing well — it's menolak kebenaran & merendahkan manusia.

This class covered ayat 1–2 only. Ayat 3–9 continue next week — this page will be updated.

2. Ayat 1 — wayl for the one who mocks

  • وَيْلٌ carries 3 meanings: kehinaan, azab, kebinasaan. When Allah says wayl, all three are gathered together.
  • Another opinion: wayl is the name of a valley in Jahannam. This is reported from some of the salaf (tafsir at-Tabari / Ibn Kathir). The hadith that says so directly (Tirmidhi 3164) is da'if — so share it as a tafsir opinion, not as the Prophet's ﷺ words. Also discussed in Surah Al-Ma'un.

Humazah vs lumazah

ViewHumazahLumazah
Ibn KathirMencela dengan kata-kataMencela dengan perbuatan
MujahidDengan tangan & mataDengan lidah
Ibn 'AbbasBoth = orang yang mencela & menjatuhkan maruah orang

Ulama differ on the exact split, and some (e.g. Ibn Jarir) treat the two words as almost synonymous. All agree on the point: it describes a habit of belittling people — behind their back or to their face.

What is ghibah?

أَتَدْرُونَ مَا الْغِيبَةُ؟ ... ذِكْرُكَ أَخَاكَ بِمَا يَكْرَهُ ... إِنْ كَانَ فِيهِ مَا تَقُولُ فَقَدِ اغْتَبْتَهُ، وَإِنْ لَمْ يَكُنْ فِيهِ فَقَدْ بَهَتَّهُ

"Tahukah kamu apa itu ghibah? … Engkau menyebut tentang saudaramu sesuatu yang dia tidak suka. … Jika perkara itu memang ada padanya, engkau telah mengumpatnya. Jika tiada, engkau telah membuat tuduhan dusta (buhtan) terhadapnya."

Muslim 2589 (Abu Hurairah) · Sahih

Being true doesn't make it okay to say. Ulama (e.g. an-Nawawi in Riyadh as-Salihin) list limited exceptions — such as reporting injustice, asking for a fatwa, or warning someone of real harm.

A sign of good Islam

مِنْ حُسْنِ إِسْلَامِ الْمَرْءِ تَرْكُهُ مَا لَا يَعْنِيهِ

"Antara tanda baiknya Islam seseorang ialah dia meninggalkan perkara yang tidak berkaitan dengannya."

Tirmidhi 2317, Ibn Majah 3976 · Hadith 40 an-Nawawi #12 · Hasan

Reflection — which one are we?

Kita ni lebih yang mana — pencela, or pengumpat? Compare that with this woman:

Ibn 'Abbas showed 'Ata' a black woman "from the people of Jannah". She had come to the Prophet ﷺ because she suffered from sawan (epilepsy), and asked him to pray for her. He said:

إِنْ شِئْتِ صَبَرْتِ وَلَكِ الْجَنَّةُ، وَإِنْ شِئْتِ دَعَوْتُ اللَّهَ أَنْ يُعَافِيَكِ

"Jika kamu mahu, bersabarlah dan bagimu syurga. Jika kamu mahu, aku doakan kepada Allah agar Dia menyembuhkanmu."

She said, "Aku bersabar." Then: "Tapi auratku tersingkap (bila sawan) — doakanlah agar ia tidak tersingkap." And he prayed for her.

Bukhari 5652, Muslim 2576 · Sahih

She chose sabar over shifa' — but still asked for one thing: that her aurat stay covered. That shows her taat & malu kepada Allah. Someone people might have looked down on, and the Prophet ﷺ pointed to her as ahli syurga.

Why the Prophet ﷺ was sent

إِنَّمَا بُعِثْتُ لِأُتَمِّمَ صَالِحَ الْأَخْلَاقِ

"Sesungguhnya aku diutus hanyalah untuk menyempurnakan akhlak yang baik."

Ahmad 8952, al-Adab al-Mufrad 273, al-Hakim 4221 · Sahih (al-Albani)

Another narration uses makarim al-akhlaq ("akhlak yang mulia") — same meaning.

3. Ayat 2 — collecting & counting

  • Mengumpul harta, then counting it again and again — this is the sifat orang bakhil.
  • The danger of cinta dunia: lupa mati, lupa akhirat. And when death comes, there's no preparation.

تَعِسَ عَبْدُ الدِّينَارِ، وَالدِّرْهَمِ، وَالْقَطِيفَةِ، وَالْخَمِيصَةِ، إِنْ أُعْطِيَ رَضِيَ، وَإِنْ لَمْ يُعْطَ لَمْ يَرْضَ

"Celakalah hamba dinar, hamba dirham, hamba kain baldu dan hamba pakaian bersulam. Jika diberi, dia reda; jika tidak diberi, dia tidak reda."

Bukhari 6435 (also 2886) · Sahih

See also Hakikat Kehidupan Dunia and Surah At-Takathur ("hartaku, hartaku").

Tadabbur lens · try this yourself

Why does Allah put the pengumpat/pencela (ayat 1) right next to the pengumpul harta (ayat 2)?

They look like two different people. But the one who mocks others feels he's lebih baik & better than others — and the one who keeps counting his harta feels he's serba cukup. Same root: istighna', feeling you don't need anyone.

كَلَّا إِنَّ الْإِنسَانَ لَيَطْغَىٰ ۝ أَن رَّآهُ اسْتَغْنَىٰ

"Ketahuilah! Sesungguhnya manusia benar-benar melampaui batas, apabila dia melihat dirinya serba cukup."

Al-'Alaq 96:6–7

How we got there:

  1. Notice the pairing — two things placed side by side in the Quran are never random.
  2. Ask what connects them — what's going on in the heart behind both actions?
  3. Let the Quran explain itself — find another ayat that names that root (here, 96:6–7).
  4. Check it — confirm the ayat on quran.com and the tafsir (e.g. Ibn Kathir) before sharing.

Your turn: next time you read a surah, look for two ideas sitting next to each other and ask, "Kenapa Allah letak dua ni sekali?"

4. Sombong — the root behind both

Feeling better than others is exactly how the Prophet ﷺ defined sombong.

لَا يَدْخُلُ الْجَنَّةَ مَنْ كَانَ فِي قَلْبِهِ مِثْقَالُ ذَرَّةٍ مِنْ كِبْرٍ

"Tidak akan masuk syurga sesiapa yang di dalam hatinya ada sebesar zarah kesombongan."

A man asked: "Seseorang suka pakaiannya cantik dan kasutnya elok?" Baginda ﷺ said:

إِنَّ اللَّهَ جَمِيلٌ يُحِبُّ الْجَمَالَ، الْكِبْرُ بَطَرُ الْحَقِّ وَغَمْطُ النَّاسِ

"Sesungguhnya Allah Maha Indah dan suka keindahan. Sombong itu ialah menolak kebenaran dan merendahkan manusia."

Muslim 91 (Ibn Mas'ud) · Sahih

Not sombong

Suka pakai cantik, kemas, bersih.

Sombong

بَطَرُ الْحَقِّ menolak kebenaran · غَمْطُ النَّاسِ merendahkan manusia

Reflections

  • Istighna' is the root. The tongue that belittles people and the hand that keeps counting harta come from the same heart — one that feels it doesn't need anyone, not even Allah.
  • The hamba dinar test: "if given, pleased; if not, displeased." How do we react when income or rezeki doesn't go our way?
  • Before we talk about someone, ask: is it beneficial? Is it my business? Would Allah be pleased with what I'm saying?
