Class Notes · Tadabbur Siri 13

Surah Al-Ma'un

Thursday, 17 September 2026  |  Surah ke-107 · 7 ayat · Makkiyyah (majority view)  |  Al-Ma'un = the small kindness
In short
  • Denying the akhirah shows in how we treat orphans and the poor.
  • The threat is to those who do pray, but carelessly and to be seen.
  • Even holding back small kindnesses is a sign of a weak deen.

"Al-Ma'un" = useful things, anything that benefits — the small kindness. The small things we hold back from others. Most scholars say the surah is Makkiyyah; some say ayat 1–3 are Makkiyyah and 4–7 Madaniyyah (about the hypocrites). It doesn't change the lesson.

2. Two parts, one root cause

Ayat 1–3 = denying the Day of Judgement → broken akhlak towards people. Ayat 4–7 = solat that's there, but empty. The root of both is the same: not being certain about the Day of Judgement.

3. The damage of denying the akhirah

When someone doesn't believe in the akhirah, the damage isn't only to them — it spreads: no sympathy, no empathy, greed, hustle, selfishness, chasing only dunia.

Even those who say they believe may not really be certain — and it shows in how little they prepare. When we plan to travel, what's our preparation? So for the akhirah, what's ours? Where's our checklist? Someone truly certain of the akhirah is willing to sacrifice dunia for it.

Yasin 36:78–79 — the disbelievers brought crumbled bones: "Siapakah yang dapat menghidupkan tulang-belulang yang telah hancur?" Allah answers: "Katakanlah: Yang menghidupkannya ialah Tuhan yang menciptakannya kali pertama, dan Dia Maha Mengetahui setiap makhluk."

Denying Allah's commands

To run a machine we need the manual. Same for understanding how this world works — the Quran and Sunnah are the Book of Guidance.

4. Denial in words — and in deeds

Denying the deen isn't just in what we say — it shows in how we treat the orphan and the poor. An orphan (yatim) = (1) has lost their father, (2) hasn't reached baligh.

A man complained to the Prophet ﷺ about the hardness of his heart. He said: "Usaplah kepala anak yatim, dan berilah makan orang miskin."

Musnad Ahmad (Abu Hurairah) · Accepted — al-Albani, Sahih al-Jami' 80; al-San'ani

The cure for a hard heart is real, practical compassion — not theory.

"Aku dan penjaga anak yatim di dalam syurga seperti ini" — and he ﷺ held up his index and middle fingers, slightly apart.

Bukhari 5304, 6005 (Sahl bin Sa'd); Muslim 2983 (Abu Hurairah) · Sahih

Be careful not to mix this up with the popular "one good deed for every hair on the orphan's head" narration — that one is da'if.

Musa & Khidir (Al-Kahf 18:71–82): a boat damaged, a boy killed, a wall rebuilt. The wall was rebuilt to protect the treasure of two orphans — because their father was righteous (18:82). Be a believer, and Allah will look after us and our families.

Action: plan how we'll actually support these groups — not just feel sorry for them.

5. Ayat 4–7 — solat that's there, but empty

Notice: the threat isn't to people who don't pray — it's to al-musallin, those who pray. This is the ayat a believer should fear.

Wayl is a word of severe punishment and destruction. Some of the salaf explained it as a valley in Jahannam (this is reported from them, e.g. in at-Tabari's tafsir) — but the hadith that says so (Tirmidhi 3164) is da'if, so it shouldn't be quoted as the Prophet's ﷺ words.

"Sahun" (lalai) — from class: heedlessness isn't only about the solat itself, but everything around it, before and after. It includes: rukuk, sujud and tuma'ninah done badly; delaying solat; men leaving the jemaah at the masjid; women being careless with their solat at home.

Al-Mu'minun 23:1–2 — "Sesungguhnya berjayalah orang-orang yang beriman, (iaitu) mereka yang khusyuk dalam solatnya." Khusyuk = the successful ones. Solat is guarded outside and inside.

Ayat 6 — riak

Showing your tears — why? Because you want people to see. There's no "winning" in front of Allah. If you want to compete, measure yourself against those who've already died: they can't add any more deeds, but we still can.

Ayat 7 — holding back small kindnesses

Withholding anything that could help someone. Small in people's eyes — but Allah mentions it as a sign of denying the deen.

6. The reward of reciting the Quran

"Dikatakan kepada pembaca Al-Quran: Bacalah dan naiklah, dan tartilkanlah sebagaimana engkau mentartilkannya di dunia — sesungguhnya kedudukanmu berada pada ayat terakhir yang engkau baca."

Abu Dawud 1464; Tirmidhi 2914 (Abdullah bin 'Amr) · Hasan sahih

The hadith speaks of recitation as the measure; understanding is still demanded, but it isn't the wording of this hadith.

Reflections

  • This surah tests iman through behaviour, not theory. How we treat orphans, whether we encourage feeding the poor, whether we share small things — these are where belief in the akhirah shows.
  • Ayat 3 condemns "not encouraging", not just "not giving". Staying silent when we could nudge someone towards good is itself a failure.
  • Where's our akhirah checklist? We plan trips in detail. The one journey that's certain is the one we leave the least prepared for.
