Surah Al-'Asr

Al-'Asr 103:1–3

2. Ayat 1 — "Demi masa"

Allah swears by time. Three possible meanings of Al-'Asr from class — and they complement each other:

  1. Waktu Asar — often the hardest solat to guard, because we're still deep in dunia's business at that hour;
  2. The lifetime of the Prophet ﷺ;
  3. Time in general — the passing of time.

3. Ayat 2 — all of mankind is in loss

Inna + la- = double emphasis. This isn't "might be at a loss" — it's certain, for mankind as a whole. Yet so many of us don't truly believe it, because we don't really know Allah as we should.

4. Ayat 3 — the only way out

Everyone starts in loss, except:

  1. Those who believe + do good deeds — both together, not one or the other; and
  2. Those who remind each other of the truth and of patience (dakwah).

What makes an amal "soleh"?

Two conditions: (1) ikhlas kerana Allah, and (2) following the sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ. Without them, deeds we thought were good can be worthless:

وَقَدِمْنَا إِلَىٰ مَا عَمِلُوا مِنْ عَمَلٍ فَجَعَلْنَاهُ هَبَاءً مَّنثُورًا

"Dan Kami tujukan kepada amal yang telah mereka kerjakan, lalu Kami jadikannya debu yang berterbangan."

Al-Furqan 25:23

Ciri Al-Abrar

Two traits of al-abrar (the righteous) from class: they give what they themselves love, and after giving, they expect nothing back. Both are straight from the Quran:

  • Ali 'Imran 3:92 — "Kamu tidak akan mencapai kebajikan (al-birr) sehingga kamu menginfakkan sebahagian daripada apa yang kamu sayangi."
  • Al-Insan 76:9 — "Sesungguhnya kami memberi makan kepada kamu hanyalah kerana Allah; kami tidak mengharapkan balasan daripada kamu dan tidak pula ucapan terima kasih."

5. Masa = modal

Time is the capital we're given in dunia to earn our reward in Jannah. The question from class: "So, bagaimana kamu guna masa kamu? Amal soleh atau tidak?"

Al-Isra' 17:9 — this Quran guides to what is most upright, and gives good news to the believers who do good deeds that theirs is a great reward.

Asking for more time — too late

"Dan belanjakanlah (dermakanlah) sebahagian daripada rezeki yang Kami berikan kepada kamu sebelum datang kematian kepada salah seorang daripada kamu, lalu dia berkata: Wahai Tuhanku, mengapa Engkau tidak menangguhkan (ajal)ku sedikit masa lagi, supaya aku dapat bersedekah dan aku termasuk dalam golongan orang yang soleh?"

Al-Munafiqun 63:10

حَتَّىٰ إِذَا جَاءَ أَحَدَهُمُ الْمَوْتُ قَالَ رَبِّ ارْجِعُونِ ۝ لَعَلِّي أَعْمَلُ صَالِحًا فِيمَا تَرَكْتُ ۚ كَلَّا

"Sehingga apabila datang kematian kepada salah seorang daripada mereka, dia berkata: Wahai Tuhanku, kembalikanlah aku, supaya aku dapat beramal soleh pada apa yang telah aku tinggalkan. Sekali-kali tidak!"

Al-Mu'minun 23:99–100

Both ask for the same thing — more time to do good — and both are refused. Capital that's been spent can't be asked for again.

Az-Zumar 39:47 — even if the wrongdoers owned everything on earth and as much again, they'd offer it all to ransom themselves from the punishment.

6. Dakwah & the levels of sabar

The second trait of those saved: reminding each other of truth and patience. Four levels of sabar from class:

  1. ⭐ Sabar + redha + syukur — the best level
  2. Sabar + hati redha
  3. Sabar, but the heart isn't redha
  4. Marah — not sabar at all

إِنَّمَا يُوَفَّى الصَّابِرُونَ أَجْرَهُم بِغَيْرِ حِسَابٍ

"Sesungguhnya orang-orang yang sabar sahaja yang akan disempurnakan pahala mereka tanpa hisab (tanpa had)."

Az-Zumar 39:10

Reflections

  • Time is the only non-renewable asset. It can't be bought back, borrowed, or inherited. Every estate plan is really a plan for managing a limited period — not just numbers.
  • 63:10 and 23:99 are the same risk as a delayed wasiat. "Nanti" is the most dangerous word in estate planning. After death there's no second chance to fix how harta is distributed.
  • 25:23 — compliance vs sincerity. A product or wasiat can be "shariah compliant" on paper and still fail on niat or substance (e.g. a wakaf for reputation). Its value in akhirat isn't measured by a compliance certificate.
  • Al-Abrar as the standard for giving: give from what's valuable to us, with a clean niat — not just leftovers, and not just for tax relief.
  • Sabar in advising. Telling people the truth about faraid and wasiat often meets resistance. The four levels of sabar are a personal gauge: can I keep giving the right advice with a calm heart, even when people aren't ready?
