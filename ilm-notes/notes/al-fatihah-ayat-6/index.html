Class Notes · Tadabbur Siri 7

Surah Al-Fatihah: Ayat 6

Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqim  |  The two kinds of hidayah
In short
  • Two kinds of hidayah: knowledge (anyone can pass on) and taufiq (only Allah gives).
  • The straight path is following the Prophet ﷺ — love is proven by ittiba'.
  • Judge by dalil, not by the extraordinary.

1. Text & meaning

2. There are two kinds of hidayah

Hidayah ilmu (irsyad wa bayan)Hidayah taufiq (amal)
Dalil 1Al-Baqarah 2:185 — هُدًى لِّلنَّاسِ "petunjuk bagi seluruh manusia"Al-Baqarah 2:2 — هُدًى لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ "petunjuk bagi orang bertakwa"
Dalil 2Asy-Syura 42:52 — "dan sesungguhnya engkau benar-benar membimbing ke jalan yang lurus"Al-Qasas 28:56 — "sesungguhnya engkau tidak dapat memberi hidayah kepada orang yang engkau sayangi, tetapi Allah memberi hidayah kepada sesiapa yang Dia kehendaki"
Who gives itThe Prophet, teachers, anyone — everyone can learn knowledgeAllah alone
Look at the dalil: two ayat about the Prophet ﷺ — one says he does guide (42:52), the other says he can't (28:56). No contradiction — two different kinds of hidayah. This is the key to the whole ayat.

They were the closest to the source of dakwah — they knew it, heard it every day — yet weren't given the taufiq to believe. Knowledge can be passed on, but only Allah opens hearts.

How does Allah give hidayah?

فَمَن يُرِدِ اللَّهُ أَن يَهْدِيَهُ يَشْرَحْ صَدْرَهُ لِلْإِسْلَامِ

"Maka sesiapa yang Allah kehendaki untuk memberinya hidayah, Dia melapangkan dadanya untuk (menerima) Islam."

Al-An'am 6:125

Hidayah isn't because of intelligence, logic, lineage or status.

3. The straight path

قُلْ إِن كُنتُمْ تُحِبُّونَ اللَّهَ فَاتَّبِعُونِي يُحْبِبْكُمُ اللَّهُ

"Katakanlah: Jika kamu mencintai Allah, maka ikutilah aku, nescaya Allah mencintai kamu."

Ali 'Imran 3:31

The straight pathNot the straight path
Following the Prophet ﷺ and his sunnahFollowing the majority · following culture · following human logic

This ayat sets the test of love: a claim of loving Allah is proven by ittiba' (following), not by feelings.

Honour with Allah

Al-Hujurat 49:13 — إِنَّ أَكْرَمَكُمْ عِندَ اللَّهِ أَتْقَاكُمْ — the most honoured of you with Allah is the most God-conscious. Bilal r.a. — a former slave, dark-skinned — was honoured to call the azan from atop the Kaabah. Islam raises people by taqwa, not race, colour or lineage.

The measure of truth is dalil — not the extraordinary

"Jika kamu melihat seseorang berjalan di atas air atau terbang di udara, janganlah kamu tertipu dengannya sehingga kamu menilai keadaannya berdasarkan Al-Quran dan As-Sunnah."

Athar (a scholar's words, not hadith) — attributed to Imam asy-Syafi'i, as quoted by Ibn Kathir in al-Bidayah wan-Nihayah; some reports attribute it originally to al-Laith bin Sa'd

4. The quality of our solat

The hadith of the man who prayed badly (al-musi' salatahu): he prayed too fast, without tuma'ninah. The Prophet ﷺ told him to repeat it three times — "go back and pray, for you have not prayed" — then taught him step by step: "Rukuklah sehingga engkau tenang dalam rukuk… sujudlah sehingga engkau tenang dalam sujud… lakukanlah demikian dalam seluruh solatmu." (Bukhari 757, 793; Muslim 397)

5. Two du'a and a warning

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عِلْمٍ لَا يَنْفَعُ، وَمِنْ قَلْبٍ لَا يَخْشَعُ، وَمِنْ نَفْسٍ لَا تَشْبَعُ، وَمِنْ دَعْوَةٍ لَا يُسْتَجَابُ لَهَا

"Ya Allah, aku berlindung kepada-Mu daripada ilmu yang tidak bermanfaat, hati yang tidak khusyuk, jiwa yang tidak pernah puas, dan doa yang tidak dimakbulkan."

Muslim 2722 (Zaid bin Arqam) · Sahih

وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ لَتَأْمُرُنَّ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ وَلَتَنْهَوُنَّ عَنِ الْمُنْكَرِ أَوْ لَيُوشِكَنَّ اللَّهُ أَنْ يَبْعَثَ عَلَيْكُمْ عِقَابًا مِنْهُ ثُمَّ تَدْعُونَهُ فَلَا يُسْتَجَابُ لَكُمْ

"Demi (Allah) yang jiwaku di tangan-Nya, hendaklah kamu menyuruh kepada yang makruf dan mencegah daripada yang mungkar, atau Allah hampir menurunkan azab daripada-Nya ke atas kamu, kemudian kamu berdoa kepada-Nya tetapi tidak dimakbulkan."

Tirmidhi 2169 (Hudhaifah bin al-Yaman) · Hasan

Notice how the last line of Muslim 2722 links to this: staying silent before wrongdoing can lead to du'a that isn't answered.

Reflections

  • We can pass on knowledge; only Allah gives taufiq. That's both a relief and a responsibility. If someone hears good advice clearly and still doesn't act, it isn't on us — Abu Talib heard the Prophet ﷺ himself for years. Our job is to be clear and sincere.
  • Information isn't what's missing. Hidayah isn't from intelligence or logic (6:125). People often know exactly what they should do; what's missing is a heart that's ready. More data rarely fixes that — du'a and patience do.
  • Form without substance. The man who prayed fast completed every pillar — yet was told "you have not prayed". Doing things quickly to tick the box isn't the same as doing them.
  • Check by dalil, not by dazzle. Impressive results, confident personalities or big names aren't proof something is right. Test it against the Quran and Sunnah.
