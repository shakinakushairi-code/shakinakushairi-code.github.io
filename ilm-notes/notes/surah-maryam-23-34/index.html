Surah Maryam: Ayat 23–34

Wednesday, 7 October 2026  |  Surah ke-19 · 98 ayat · Makkiyyah  |  Maryam gives birth, and Nabi Isa a.s. speaks from the cradle
In short
  • Maryam, alone in labour, was told: don't grieve, shake the palm, eat, stay silent.
  • Nabi Isa's first words from the cradle: "I am the servant of Allah."
  • He wasn't killed — he was raised, and will return before the Hour.

1. About the surah

MatterDetails
PositionSurah 19; after Al-Kahf, before Taha · Juz 16
Order of revelation44th — after Fatir, before Taha
Named afterThe story of Maryam and her son Nabi Isa a.s. (ayat 16–33)
Main purposeDeclaring Allah free of partners, wife or child; He alone is Creator and Giver (19:35, 19:88–92)
Special featureThe surah that mentions Ar-Rahman the most — 16 times

Structure: ayat 1 opening (five letters) · Part 1 (2–65) Allah's mercy on the prophets — Zakariyya & Yahya, Maryam & Isa (16–40), Ibrahim, Musa and others · Part 2 (66–95) answering the musyrikin's doubts · ayat 96–98 closing.

Maryam's rank: the Prophet ﷺ drew four lines in the ground and said the best women of Jannah are Khadijah binti Khuwailid, Fatimah binti Muhammad, Asiah binti Muzahim (wife of Firaun) and Maryam binti 'Imran (Musnad Ahmad 2668, 2901 · sahih isnad, Ahmad Syakir & al-Arna'ut).

Maryam 19:23–34

3. Maryam's story in 3 phases

  1. Phase 1 — Jibril comes (19:16–22): Maryam will conceive.
  2. Phase 2 — giving birth to Isa (19:23–26): the focus of this class — four instructions to Maryam.
  3. Phase 3 — returning to her people (19:27–33).

19:23 — "If only I had died before this"

Maryam wished for death because she imagined her name being shamed — she wanted to be something forgotten and never remembered. Lesson: sometimes we do speak from emotion.

Ibn Kathir: she knew she'd be tested and her people wouldn't believe her. This isn't a general licence — the Prophet ﷺ forbade wishing for death because of harm; if you must, say: "Ya Allah, hidupkanlah aku selagi hidup itu baik bagiku, dan wafatkanlah aku apabila wafat itu baik bagiku" (Bukhari 5671). Maryam's fear was for her deen and honour, not a worldly harm.

Phase 2 — four instructions

19:24 — who called out to her?

A valid difference among the salaf, both quoted by Ibn Kathir:

ViewHeld byReasoning
JibrilIbn 'Abbas, ad-Dahhak, Qatadah, as-Suddi and othersIsa didn't speak until Maryam brought him to her people
Nabi IsaMujahid, al-Hasan, Ibn Zayd; chosen by at-TabariMaryam pointed straight to her baby (19:29), as if she already knew he could speak. See also Ali 'Imran 3:46: "dia akan berkata-kata kepada manusia ketika dalam buaian"

The two qira'at support both readings: min tahtiha (from beneath her) and man tahtaha (the one beneath her). Sariyya = a stream, per the majority — Ibn Kathir strengthens this because the next ayat mentions eating and drinking.

19:25 — shake the trunk

Allah wants to teach us to make an effort and move, even a little — even at her weakest moment, alone and in labour. Grammar note: in ilaiki and 'alaiki, ـكِ (ki) is feminine; ـكَ (ka) is masculine.

"Sesiapa yang bersarapan setiap pagi dengan tujuh biji kurma 'ajwah, racun dan sihir tidak akan memudaratkannya pada hari itu."

Bukhari 5445 (Sa'd bin Abi Waqqas) · Sahih

Note: the ayat mentions rutab (fresh dates); the hadith mentions 'ajwah — two separate dalil about dates, so don't merge them as if Maryam ate 'ajwah.

19:26 — the vow of silence

Sauman here means silence (Anas, Ibn 'Abbas) — in their syariat, fasting included refraining from speech. In our syariat a vow of silence isn't prescribed: the Prophet ﷺ told a man who vowed to stand, not take shade, not speak and fast — to speak, take shade, sit, and only complete his fast (Bukhari 6704). The hikmah: Maryam didn't need to defend herself — her defence came from Allah, through the tongue of her baby.

Phase 3 — 19:27–29: her people confront her

Maryam stays silent because of her vow, and points to her baby. The people ask how they can talk to a baby in the cradle (al-mahd).

"Sister of Harun" doesn't mean the biological sister of Nabi Harun a.s. When Christians of Najran raised this, the Prophet ﷺ explained that people used to name their children after the prophets and righteous people before them (Muslim 2135). "Maryam binti 'Imran" is stated in At-Tahrim 66:12.

4. Nabi Isa a.s. — ten declarations from the cradle

Nabi Isa is the 24th of the 25 prophets and messengers named in the Quran (by the usual teaching order).

Ibn Kathir: Isa's very first words were an admission of servitude — and at the same time they cleared his mother's name. Mubarak = a teacher of good, someone of great benefit (Mujahid).

Isa's Book and mission

  • Ali 'Imran 3:48 — Allah taught Isa writing, wisdom, the Taurat and the Injil — so he mastered the Taurat too.
  • Ali 'Imran 3:50 — he confirmed the Taurat and made lawful some things previously forbidden — an "update" to the earlier syariat.
  • Al-Ma'idah 5:46 — sent following the prophets of Bani Israil, confirming the Taurat, given the Injil with guidance and light.
  • As-Saff 61:6 — Isa gave good news of a messenger after him named Ahmad — one of the names of Nabi Muhammad ﷺ (Bukhari 3532).
"Taurat = Old Testament, Injil = New Testament"? Only as a rough analogy. The Taurat was revealed to Nabi Musa and the Injil to Nabi Isa. Today's Old Testament is a collection of many books, not just the Taurat; the New Testament is four Gospels written by later authors plus letters — not the Injil revealed to Isa. And after the Quran came, it became the muhaimin — guardian and measure — over the earlier books (Al-Ma'idah 5:48).

Maryam and Isa protected from shaitan's touch

وَإِنِّي أُعِيذُهَا بِكَ وَذُرِّيَّتَهَا مِنَ الشَّيْطَانِ الرَّجِيمِ

"…dan aku memohon perlindungan-Mu untuknya dan zuriatnya daripada syaitan yang direjam." — the du'a of 'Imran's wife, Maryam's mother.

Ali 'Imran 3:36

Every child of Adam is touched by shaitan at birth and cries out from it — except Maryam and her son (Bukhari 3431; Muslim 2366). Abu Hurairah then recited 3:36. So it's two people, Maryam and Isa — not Isa alone.

أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّةِ مِنْ كُلِّ شَيْطَانٍ وَهَامَّةٍ، وَمِنْ كُلِّ عَيْنٍ لَامَّةٍ

"Aku berlindung dengan kalimah-kalimah Allah yang sempurna daripada setiap syaitan dan binatang berbisa, dan daripada setiap mata yang jahat." — the du'a the Prophet ﷺ made for al-Hasan and al-Husain, saying Nabi Ibrahim made it for Ismail and Ishaq. One we can make for our own children.

Bukhari 3371 (Ibn 'Abbas) · Sahih

5. The return of Nabi Isa and the signs of the Hour

The ten major signs (Muslim 2901, Hudhaifah bin Asid): the smoke, Dajjal, the Beast, the sun rising from the west, the descent of Isa son of Maryam, Ya'juj and Ma'juj, three landslides (east, west, Arabian Peninsula), and a fire from Yemen driving people to the gathering place.

The sequence of events (Muslim 2937, an-Nawwas bin Sam'an):

  1. Dajjal appears between Syam and Iraq; he stays on earth 40 days.
  2. Isa descends at the white minaret east of Damascus, his hands on the wings of two angels.
  3. Isa pursues Dajjal and kills him at the gate of Ludd.
  4. Allah tells Isa to take His servants to shelter at at-Tur.
  5. Ya'juj and Ma'juj come out; Isa and his companions are besieged and make du'a.
  6. Allah sends worms on their necks — they all die at once; birds carry the bodies away and rain washes the earth.
  7. The earth is told: أَنْبِتِي ثَمَرَتَكِ وَرُدِّي بَرَكَتَكِ — "bring forth your fruit and restore your blessing."
  8. A gentle wind takes the soul of every believer; only the worst of people remain, and the Hour comes upon them.

Isa stays on earth 40 years, then dies and the Muslims pray over him (Abu Dawud 4324 · sahih, al-Albani, Ibn Hibban, Ahmad Syakir). Muslim 2940 mentions people living seven years without enmity after Dajjal — both are sahih; how scholars reconcile them is worth asking the ustaz.

Nabi Isa's appearance

Medium height, reddish-white complexion, straight hair (Bukhari 3239); reddish, curly hair, broad chest (Bukhari 3438); Ibn 'Umar's narration of the Prophet's ﷺ dream describes a man of brown complexion with straight hair, water dripping from his head (Bukhari 3441). "Medium height" repeats; details of skin and hair differ between sahih narrations — so mention which narration when sharing.

6. 19:34 and An-Nisa 4:155–159 — closing the story

  • 19:34 — that is Isa: his qualities, his true story.
  • 4:156 — because of their disbelief and their great slander against Maryam.
  • 4:157 — and their claim "we killed the Messiah" — they did not kill him nor crucify him; it was made to appear so to them.
  • 4:158 — rather, Allah raised him up to Himself.
  • 4:159 — every one of the People of the Book will believe in him before his death.
Putting 19:33 ("the day I die"), 4:158–159 and Abu Dawud 4324 together: Nabi Isa has not yet died — he was raised, will descend, and then die. And the surah itself closes the point: 19:35 — it is not befitting for Allah to take a son, glory be to Him; 19:36 — "Allah is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him. This is the straight path."

Reflections

  • 19:25 — shake first, then the dates fall. At her weakest, Maryam was still told to move the trunk. Rezeki is from Allah, but the means must still be moved. Tawakkal isn't sitting still.
  • 19:31 — "as long as I live" and "wherever I am". Two measures at once: obligations that run our whole life, and benefit that follows us wherever we go. Is what we have benefiting people around us now?
  • 19:28 — a good name is part of what we leave behind. Her people argued from her parents' reputation. Like the wall of the two orphans (18:82), what we pass on isn't only wealth — it's our name, our du'a (3:36) and how we raised our children.
  • 19:33 — three moments that are certain. Born, die, raised. We should prepare for the second with the third in mind.
