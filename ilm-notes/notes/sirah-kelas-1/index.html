Ibadah & Tarbiyah Awal

The early worship & tarbiyah of Rasulullah ﷺ  |  Tuesday, 29 September 2026  |  Fasa Makkah Awal  |  Kitab: As-Sirah an-Nabawiyyah, Dr. Ali Muhammad al-Sallabi, Bab 2 (Turunnya Wahyu & Dakwah Secara Rahsia)
In short
  • Sirah is Allah's tarbiyah curriculum, not just history.
  • With the right niat, our whole day can be ibadah.
  • Allah built the Prophet's ﷺ connection at night first; khusyuk matters most.

1. Why learn Sirah?

So that we worship the way Rasulullah ﷺ did. Allah educated the Prophet ﷺ and the sahabat through the events of the sirah — so sirah isn't just history. It's a tarbiyah curriculum that Allah designed.

2. Ibadah starts with thinking

Reflecting on the creation of the heavens and the earth is itself ibadah — those who do it are the ulul albab (Ali 'Imran 3:190–191). But human knowledge is very limited: "Kamu tidak diberi ilmu melainkan sedikit" (Al-Isra' 17:85). We need to tell apart ilmu manusia and ilmu Al-Quran.

The problem: since young, we're taught to know dunia and love dunia — not to know Allah.

Mafatih al-Ghayb — the keys of the unseen

وَعِندَهُ مَفَاتِحُ الْغَيْبِ لَا يَعْلَمُهَا إِلَّا هُوَ

"Dan di sisi-Nya kunci-kunci segala yang ghaib; tiada yang mengetahuinya melainkan Dia."

Al-An'am 6:59

Picture the ghaib as a locked treasury — and the keys are only in Allah's hand. The same ayat says not a single leaf falls, in darkness or light, except that Allah knows it. The Prophet ﷺ explained there are five keys (Bukhari 4697; Luqman 31:34):

  1. When the Hour will come
  2. When rain will fall
  3. What is in the womb
  4. What a person will earn tomorrow
  5. In which land a person will die

All five touch every human life: the end of the world, rezeki, birth, the future, and death.

"But ultrasound can tell the gender, and forecasts predict rain?" Allah knows everything about what's in the womb — its ajal, rezeki, whether it'll be happy or wretched. Ultrasound only sees what's already formed and observable — and what can be seen is no longer ghaib. Weather forecasts are probability from data, not certainty. Allah's knowledge is absolute, and known before anything happens.

3. What is ibadah?

The foundation: "Janganlah kamu sembah melainkan hanya kepada-Nya" (Al-Isra' 17:23). Ibn Taymiyyah's definition:

الْعِبَادَةُ اسْمٌ جَامِعٌ لِكُلِّ مَا يُحِبُّهُ اللَّهُ وَيَرْضَاهُ مِنَ الْأَقْوَالِ وَالْأَعْمَالِ الظَّاهِرَةِ وَالْبَاطِنَةِ

"Ibadah ialah nama yang merangkumi segala yang Allah cintai dan redhai, sama ada perkataan atau perbuatan, zahir dan batin."

Ibn Taymiyyah, Majmu' al-Fatawa 10/149

It's a chain: ↑ ibadah → ↑ hati suci → ↑ dekat dengan Allah.

Ibadah khusus: taharah & solat

Taharah — purifying ourselves from hadas besar and kecil, with water, or tayammum with dust if there's no water (Al-Ma'idah 5:6). Solat is the line between Islam and kufur: "Antara seseorang dengan syirik dan kufur ialah meninggalkan solat" (Muslim 82).

What if there's no water and no dust? (Faqid at-Tahurain) — e.g. someone trapped, or so ill they can't do wudhu' or tayammum. Ulama differ:

ViewHeld by
Solat in that state, then must repeatMazhab Syafi'i (muktamad), Abu Yusuf (Hanafi)
Solat in that state, no need to repeatMazhab Hanbali, Asyhab (Maliki), al-Muzani (Syafi'i)
Don't solat, qada laterAbu Hanifah, one narration from Malik
Don't solat, no qadaMazhab Maliki (muktamad)

The view considered strongest in evidence is to pray and not repeat. An-Nawawi — a great Syafi'i scholar himself — said al-Muzani's view has the strongest dalil. The basis is the story of Aisyah r.a.'s necklace: some sahabat prayed without wudhu', and the Prophet ﷺ neither objected nor told them to repeat (Bukhari 336). They did what they could — just like someone who can't stand prays sitting.

Ibadah umum: it's all about niat

  • Even a smile is ibadah: "Senyummu kepada saudaramu adalah sedekah" (Tirmidhi 1956 · sahih, al-Albani). Leaving what's bad is also ibadah.
  • "Setiap amalan bergantung pada niat" (Bukhari 1).
  • Even a morsel of food you put in your spouse's mouth is rewarded, if it's for Allah (Bukhari 56).
  • Football can be ibadah — if aurat is covered, it doesn't clash with solat times, and you don't lose your temper when you miss a goal.

A student asked: is inviting a friend to exercise ibadah?

The ustaz asked back: why do you exercise? The niat decides its value.

  • If it's so your body is strong for worship, that's a good niat.
  • But the test: once you're strong, is your solat khusyuk?
  • Compare with someone with bad knees who has to pray sitting — they might be far more khusyuk.
  • Lesson: physical strength is just a tool. What Allah values is the heart.

Supporting dalil: "The strong believer is better and more beloved to Allah than the weak believer, and in both there is good" (Muslim 2664); "Allah doesn't look at your appearance and wealth, but at your hearts and deeds" (Muslim 2564); those who are ill may pray sitting or lying down (Bukhari 1117), with no loss in its value.

Kesimpulan: we can actually be in ibadah 24 hours a day.

Why does ibadah feel heavy?

حُفَّتِ الْجَنَّةُ بِالْمَكَارِهِ، وَحُفَّتِ النَّارُ بِالشَّهَوَاتِ

"Syurga dikelilingi perkara-perkara yang dibenci (nafsu), dan neraka dikelilingi syahwat."

Muslim 2822 (Anas bin Malik) · Sahih

Everything good, the nafsu dislikes — and the reverse. The roh is purified through ibadah: solat, reading Al-Quran, constant zikir and tasbih. When it isn't, the connection with Allah breaks.

5. Al-Muzzammil — why rise at night?

Sleeping little doesn't make sense to human logic. But for the Prophet ﷺ, qiyamullail at that time was a compulsory command from Allah — even before most of the Quran had come down. Why?

(a) The Quran is heavy. "Kami akan turunkan kepadamu perkataan yang berat" (73:5). Even in dunia: when do we feel satisfied? When we sit the exam and get the result we hoped for. Becoming a lawyer has a process too. Getting into Jannah is the same — that's why the Quran is heavy.

(b) The night purifies the heart. Revelation touches the heart, purifies it, and makes us more ikhlas. The night isn't disturbed by dunia (73:6).

(c) The tarbiyah programme of Al-Muzzammil:

#PracticeAyat
1Solat malam73:1–4
2Tartil Al-Quran73:4
3Zikrullah + tabattul (devote yourself to worship, cutting off dunia for a while)73:8
4Tawakkal — take Allah as your Wakil73:9
5Sabar73:10

(d) Why was solat made obligatory so early? To show how important solat and our connection with Allah are. Qiyamullail was obligatory first, done by the Prophet ﷺ and the sahabat for a year, then became sunat (Muslim 746). The five daily prayers were made fardu later, on the night of Isra' Mi'raj (Bukhari 349).

What matters most? Khusyuk. The night prayer is a pure time that trains us to be khusyuk and to understand — so we can act in the day and actually feel the five daily prayers. Understanding Arabic matters for understanding the Quran. So, belajarlah!

6. Signs of the successful believer

Al-Mu'minun 23:1–3 — the believers have succeeded: those who are khusyuk in their solat, and who turn away from what's sia-sia (laghw). Ayat 4–11 continue: zakat, guarding chastity, amanah, keeping promises, guarding solat.

As-Sajdah 32:15–17

(a) When reminded, they fall in sujud straight away (32:15) — immediate obedience, no bargaining.

The ustaz's test: Allah promises a great reward for those who forgive: "Sesiapa yang memaafkan dan berbuat baik, maka pahalanya atas (tanggungan) Allah" (Asy-Syura 42:40). Do we forgive immediately the person who wronged us badly? If we truly understood the ayat, we'd obey straight away — even if the heart is heavy.

From the Sirah — Abu Bakr r.a. and Mistah. Mistah, a poor relative whom Abu Bakr supported, took part in spreading the slander against Aisyah r.a. — Abu Bakr's own daughter. Abu Bakr swore never to help him again. Then An-Nur 24:22 came down: "Tidakkah kamu suka Allah mengampunimu?" Abu Bakr answered at once: "Bahkan, demi Allah, aku suka Allah mengampuniku" — and resumed his support (Bukhari 4750).

(b) They leave their beds (32:16), calling on their Lord in fear and hope — hoping for His reward and Jannah. Not tamak duit!

(c) The best gift is a surprise (32:17). No soul knows what joy is hidden for them.

Hadis qudsi: "Aku telah sediakan untuk hamba-hamba-Ku yang soleh apa yang tidak pernah dilihat oleh mata, tidak pernah didengar oleh telinga, dan tidak pernah terlintas di hati manusia."

Bukhari 4779 · Sahih

Want something precious? Sacrifice something precious. The most precious thing is time — and that includes our sleep.

Summary

  1. Ibadah = everything Allah loves and is pleased with. With the right niat, 24 hours can be ibadah.
  2. Allah built the Prophet's ﷺ personal connection (qiyamullail) first, before public responsibility.
  3. The Quran is heavy, so it needs preparation: the night, tartil, zikir, tawakkal, sabar.
  4. Khusyuk matters more than physical strength or perfection.
  5. Understanding an ayat means obeying straight away — including forgiving.
  6. The greatest reward is hidden, so that only the ikhlas work for it.
