2. Ayat 1 — bermegah dengan 'banyak'

  • 'Banyak' can be anything: duit, pangkat, kedudukan, ilmu, ramai anak.
  • This surah condemns that attitude. We rarely show off when we have one — it's when we have many that we start to berbangga. And that will surely make us lalai.
  • Lalai from zikrullah, which means two things: (a) mentaati Allah, and (b) mengingati Allah.
  • Every nikmat is a test; anak itu 'fitnah' = ujian.

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَا تُلْهِكُمْ أَمْوَالُكُمْ وَلَا أَوْلَادُكُمْ عَن ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ ۚ وَمَن يَفْعَلْ ذَٰلِكَ فَأُولَٰئِكَ هُمُ الْخَاسِرُونَ

"Wahai orang-orang yang beriman, janganlah harta kamu dan anak-anak kamu melalaikan kamu daripada mengingati Allah. Sesiapa yang berbuat demikian, maka mereka itulah orang-orang yang rugi."

Al-Munafiqun 63:9

In class: this applies to harta and anak-anak — and cucu too.

وَمَا الْحَيَاةُ الدُّنْيَا إِلَّا لَعِبٌ وَلَهْوٌ ۖ وَلَلدَّارُ الْآخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ لِّلَّذِينَ يَتَّقُونَ ۗ أَفَلَا تَعْقِلُونَ

"Dan tidaklah kehidupan dunia ini melainkan permainan dan hiburan yang melalaikan; dan sesungguhnya negeri akhirat itu lebih baik bagi orang-orang yang bertakwa. Tidakkah kamu mahu berfikir?"

Al-An'am 6:32

  • لَعِبٌ (la'ib) = main-main — no real benefit, no faedah. لَهْوٌ (lahw) = something that distracts and melalaikan.

3. Ayat 2 — 'menziarahi' kubur

  • The word ziarah tells us the grave is just a persinggahan — a visit, not a forever place. A visitor always moves on.
  • So why are we so busy with la'ib that's temporary? Even dunia is short compared to alam kubur, then Padang Mahsyar, and what comes after…
  • This is the well-known tafsir. A minority view says it means they kept boasting until they even counted the dead in their graves — both fit the text.

إِنَّ الْقَبْرَ أَوَّلُ مَنْزِلٍ مِنْ مَنَازِلِ الآخِرَةِ، فَإِنْ نَجَا مِنْهُ فَمَا بَعْدَهُ أَيْسَرُ مِنْهُ، وَإِنْ لَمْ يَنْجُ مِنْهُ فَمَا بَعْدَهُ أَشَدُّ مِنْهُ

"Sesungguhnya kubur ialah persinggahan pertama daripada persinggahan-persinggahan akhirat. Jika seseorang selamat daripadanya, maka apa yang selepasnya lebih mudah; dan jika dia tidak selamat daripadanya, maka apa yang selepasnya lebih dahsyat."

Tirmidhi 2308 · Hasan

Uthman bin Affan r.a. used to weep until his beard was wet whenever he stood at a grave — and he would quote the hadith above. This was a man promised Jannah, one of the ten (Tirmidhi 3747). Even orang beriman yang dijanjikan syurga pun memikirkan kematian. What about us?

4. Ayat 3–4 — kenapa diulang?

  • Repetition for emphasis: you will know, you will see — a "told you so".
  • In class: ayat 3 = you'll see it when you die; ayat 4 = in the grave, you'll realise and see it again.

Ulama differ here: (a) it's purely emphasis (ta'kid); (b) ayat 3 = at death / in the grave, ayat 4 = at the resurrection (quoted by Ibn Kathir). The ustaz's explanation is close to view (b).

5. Ayat 5–7 — tingkatan yakin

Ayat 5 · 'Ilm al-yaqin

Tahu dengan ilmu — the knowledge enters the heart.

Ayat 7 · 'Ayn al-yaqin

Lihat dengan mata sendiri — you see it for real.

  • The third level, Haqq al-yaqin (experiencing it yourself), was also mentioned in class. Note: it isn't in this surah — the phrase appears in Al-Waqi'ah 56:95 and Al-Haqqah 69:51.
  • Ayat 6 — Neraka Jahim. Jahim = blazing, menyala-nyala; Jahannam = deep and dark. Both are names for the same Hell, describing different aspects of it.
Ayat 5 says: kalaulah kamu tahu dengan ilmu yang yakin. Meaning — kalau kita betul-betul yakin, we wouldn't be living the way we do. Tau macam tu je tak cukup.

6. Ayat 8 — ditanya tentang nikmat

  • Every nikmat will be asked about: hobbies, collections, ilmu, solat. Did we reflect on them? Did we do zikir pagi-petang? Did we tadabbur?

Nabi ﷺ, Abu Bakr dan Umar keluar kerana lapar, lalu dijamu kurma, air dan daging oleh seorang Ansar. Selepas makan, Baginda bersabda:

لَتُسْأَلُنَّ عَنْ هَذَا النَّعِيمِ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ

"Kamu pasti akan ditanya tentang nikmat ini pada hari kiamat."

Muslim 2038 (Abu Hurairah) · Sahih

One simple meal, eaten when they were genuinely hungry — and still it'll be asked about. Imagine everything else we have.

7. Bersedia untuk mati

رَبَّنَا أَبْصَرْنَا وَسَمِعْنَا فَارْجِعْنَا نَعْمَلْ صَالِحًا إِنَّا مُوقِنُونَ

"(Mereka berkata): Wahai Tuhan kami, kami telah melihat dan mendengar, maka kembalikanlah kami (ke dunia) supaya kami beramal soleh; sesungguhnya kami sekarang orang-orang yang yakin."

As-Sajdah 32:12

Notice — they ask to go back to do amal soleh, not to make more money. So why are we chasing dunia now?

وَلَوْ رُدُّوا لَعَادُوا لِمَا نُهُوا عَنْهُ وَإِنَّهُمْ لَكَاذِبُونَ

"Dan kalaulah mereka dikembalikan (ke dunia), nescaya mereka akan mengulangi apa yang dilarang daripadanya, dan sesungguhnya mereka adalah pendusta."

Al-An'am 6:28

حَتَّىٰ إِذَا جَاءَ أَحَدَهُمُ الْمَوْتُ قَالَ رَبِّ ارْجِعُونِ ۝ لَعَلِّي أَعْمَلُ صَالِحًا فِيمَا تَرَكْتُ ۚ كَلَّا ۚ إِنَّهَا كَلِمَةٌ هُوَ قَائِلُهَا ۖ وَمِن وَرَائِهِم بَرْزَخٌ إِلَىٰ يَوْمِ يُبْعَثُونَ

"Sehingga apabila datang kematian kepada salah seorang daripada mereka, dia berkata: Wahai Tuhanku, kembalikanlah aku, supaya aku dapat beramal soleh pada apa yang telah aku tinggalkan. Sekali-kali tidak! Itu hanyalah kata-kata yang diucapkannya. Dan di hadapan mereka ada barzakh hingga hari mereka dibangkitkan."

Al-Mu'minun 23:99–100

Kematian boleh datang bila-bila masa. There's no going back to fix things.

8. "Hartaku, hartaku" — what's actually ours

يَقُولُ الْعَبْدُ: مَالِي مَالِي، إِنَّمَا لَهُ مِنْ مَالِهِ ثَلَاثٌ: مَا أَكَلَ فَأَفْنَى، أَوْ لَبِسَ فَأَبْلَى، أَوْ أَعْطَى فَاقْتَنَى، وَمَا سِوَى ذَلِكَ فَهُوَ ذَاهِبٌ وَتَارِكُهُ لِلنَّاسِ

"Seorang hamba berkata: 'Hartaku, hartaku!' Sedangkan hartanya yang sebenar hanya tiga: apa yang dia makan lalu habis, apa yang dia pakai lalu lusuh, atau apa yang dia berikan (sedekah) lalu tersimpan (untuk akhirat). Selain itu, ia akan pergi dan ditinggalkan untuk orang lain."

Muslim 2959 (Abu Hurairah) · Sahih

The narration linked directly to this surah is Muslim 2958 ('Abdullah bin asy-Syikhkhir): he came to the Prophet ﷺ while he was reciting Alhakumut-takathur, and Baginda said, "Anak Adam berkata: hartaku, hartaku…" — with the same meaning.

Reflections

  • Muslim 2959 is the most honest audit of our wealth: what we accumulate will literally be ditinggalkan untuk orang lain. What's truly ours is only what we used for our needs, and what we sent ahead — sedekah, wakaf.
  • Takathur isn't about having a lot — it's about being lalai because of it. The test: does more harta / ilmu / followers increase our zikrullah (taat + ingat), or replace it?
  • Ayat 8 + Muslim 2038: every nikmat will be asked about. Kena ada syukur yang sepadan — not just enjoying it.
  • Preparing for death (32:12, 23:99) isn't just about what we leave behind — it's the amal that stays with us after everything else has been distributed.
