Class Notes · Sirah Nabi ﷺ · Kelas 2

Solat: Jalan Menyucikan Jiwa

Tuesday, 13 October 2026  |  Ustaz Abd Muein  |  Fasa Makkah Awal  |  Kitab: As-Sirah an-Nabawiyyah, al-Sallabi, Bab 2, m/s 161
In short
  • Solat purifies the soul — it should be what satisfies us most.
  • Rezeki is guaranteed; chase taqwa, and seek rezeki to give, not hoard.
  • Solat brings closeness (sujud), conversation (munajat), and rest.

1. Continuing: purifying the soul

Last class: ibadah is khusus and umum, and reflecting on Allah's creation is also ibadah. This class focuses on solat — one of the main ways to cleanse the heart, fardu or sunat.

فَوَيْلٌ لِّلْقَاسِيَةِ قُلُوبُهُم مِّن ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ

"Maka celakalah bagi mereka yang keras hatinya daripada mengingati Allah."

Az-Zumar 39:22

Solat wipes away the dirt of the heart: "Dirikanlah solat pada kedua-dua tepi siang dan pada sebahagian malam. Sesungguhnya kebaikan menghapuskan keburukan" (Hud 11:114).

2. Night prayer — the honour of a believer

From the very start, before the five daily prayers, Allah commanded the Prophet ﷺ and the sahabat to pray tahajjud. Its reward: a praised station (maqaman mahmuda), Al-Isra' 17:79.

شَرَفُ الْمُؤْمِنِ قِيَامُ اللَّيْلِ

Jibril berkata kepada Nabi ﷺ: "Kemuliaan seorang mukmin ialah pada solat malamnya."

At-Tabarani, al-Awsat 4278 · Hasan (al-Albani, Sahih al-Jami' 73)

3. Ta-Ha 20:130–132 — sabar, solat, redha

(a) Ayat 130: sabar and solat

فَاصْبِرْ عَلَىٰ مَا يَقُولُونَ وَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِ رَبِّكَ قَبْلَ طُلُوعِ الشَّمْسِ وَقَبْلَ غُرُوبِهَا ۖ وَمِنْ آنَاءِ اللَّيْلِ فَسَبِّحْ وَأَطْرَافَ النَّهَارِ لَعَلَّكَ تَرْضَىٰ

"Maka bersabarlah atas apa yang mereka katakan, dan bertasbihlah dengan memuji Tuhanmu sebelum terbit matahari dan sebelum terbenamnya, dan pada waktu-waktu malam serta di hujung-hujung siang, supaya engkau redha."

Ta-Ha 20:130

  • Sabar: when people mocked what the Prophet ﷺ conveyed, Allah said be patient — and the medicine is solat.
  • The prayer times in this ayat: before sunrise (Subuh), before sunset (Asar), the night (Maghrib & Isyak), and the ends of the day.
  • La'allaka tarda — "so that you are redha", i.e. satisfied. In daily life, solat should be the thing that makes us feel satisfied and redha.

وَجُعِلَتْ قُرَّةُ عَيْنِي فِي الصَّلَاةِ

"…dan dijadikan penyejuk mataku (kepuasanku) dalam solat."

An-Nasa'i 3939 (Anas) · Hasan

(b) Ayat 131: don't be dazzled by dunia

وَلَا تَمُدَّنَّ عَيْنَيْكَ إِلَىٰ مَا مَتَّعْنَا بِهِ أَزْوَاجًا مِّنْهُمْ زَهْرَةَ الْحَيَاةِ الدُّنْيَا لِنَفْتِنَهُمْ فِيهِ ۚ وَرِزْقُ رَبِّكَ خَيْرٌ وَأَبْقَىٰ

"Dan janganlah engkau panjangkan pandangan matamu kepada kesenangan yang Kami berikan kepada beberapa golongan mereka, sebagai bunga kehidupan dunia, untuk Kami uji mereka padanya. Dan rezeki Tuhanmu lebih baik dan lebih kekal."

Ta-Ha 20:131

  • Dunia is like a flower (zahrah): beautiful, but it wilts fast. Don't be fooled.
  • The command is to the Prophet ﷺ, but the lesson is for his ummah.
  • "Rezeki Tuhanmu" isn't just wealth — it includes iman, khusyuk, ilmu, amanah: a good life (hayatan tayyibah, An-Nahl 16:97).

(c) Ayat 132: rezeki is guaranteed — chase taqwa

وَأْمُرْ أَهْلَكَ بِالصَّلَاةِ وَاصْطَبِرْ عَلَيْهَا ۖ لَا نَسْأَلُكَ رِزْقًا ۖ نَّحْنُ نَرْزُقُكَ ۗ وَالْعَاقِبَةُ لِلتَّقْوَىٰ

"Dan perintahkanlah keluargamu mendirikan solat dan bersabarlah dalam menjaganya. Kami tidak meminta rezeki daripadamu; Kamilah yang memberi rezeki kepadamu. Dan kesudahan yang baik adalah bagi orang yang bertakwa."

Ta-Ha 20:132

Don't fear what Allah has already guaranteed (rezeki). Fear not doing what He commanded. Our goal is to be among the muttaqin — the Quran itself says the good ending belongs to them.

4. The sahabat — servants of Allah, not of nafsu

Solat is the mukadimah to every other command. The sahabat's hearts were purified, so when Allah's commands came, they followed easily.

وَالَّذِينَ اسْتَجَابُوا لِرَبِّهِمْ وَأَقَامُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَأَمْرُهُمْ شُورَىٰ بَيْنَهُمْ وَمِمَّا رَزَقْنَاهُمْ يُنفِقُونَ

"Dan orang-orang yang menyahut perintah Tuhan mereka, mendirikan solat, urusan mereka diputuskan dengan bermesyuarat, dan mereka menginfakkan sebahagian rezeki yang Kami berikan."

Asy-Syura 42:38

AttributeMeaning
Menyahut perintahObey without delay
SolatThe foundation of the connection with Allah
SyuraDiscuss, accept others' views
InfaqGive from what Allah has given

Infaq — rezeki is for giving, not just collecting

Allah mentions infaq many times in the Quran — but the Quran never tells us to work hard just to accumulate. Rezeki is given by Allah, not purely the result of our effort.

How dunia teaches usHow the Quran teaches us
Cari rezeki → tambah → kumpulCari rezeki → infaq

We rarely do the second because dunia trained us otherwise. Yet infaq is a road to Jannah: it's prepared for the muttaqin, "iaitu orang yang menginfakkan hartanya ketika senang dan susah" (Ali 'Imran 3:133–134).

Critical thinking — so no need to try? No. The Quran still tells us to seek rezeki: "bertebaranlah di muka bumi dan carilah kurnia Allah" (Al-Jumu'ah 62:10; also Al-Mulk 67:15). The Prophet ﷺ described those who truly rely on Allah as birds: they leave hungry in the morning and return full in the evening (Tirmidhi 2344 · hasan sahih) — but the bird still goes out. Effort is a must; the heart depends on Allah, not on the effort. And the purpose of seeking rezeki is to obey and to give, not to hoard.

5. The Quran's rights over us

Baca → Faham → Amal. "Kitab yang Kami turunkan kepadamu, penuh keberkatan, supaya mereka mentadabbur ayat-ayatnya dan supaya orang yang berakal mengambil peringatan" (Sad 38:29).

The ustaz added: a husband's responsibility to his wife isn't just nafkah but also ilmu — "Peliharalah dirimu dan keluargamu daripada api neraka" (At-Tahrim 66:6).

6. Three things solat gives us

① Closest to Allah — in sujud

أَقْرَبُ مَا يَكُونُ الْعَبْدُ مِنْ رَبِّهِ وَهُوَ سَاجِدٌ فَأَكْثِرُوا الدُّعَاءَ

"Saat paling dekat seorang hamba dengan Tuhannya ialah ketika dia sujud, maka perbanyakkanlah doa."

Muslim 482 (Abu Hurairah) · Sahih

So make lots of du'a in sujud — not only after salam. Du'a after salam is still sunnah (e.g. the Prophet ﷺ taught Mu'adh to say at the end of every prayer "Allahumma a'inni 'ala dhikrika wa shukrika wa husni 'ibadatik" — Abu Dawud 1522, sahih). The point: don't save every du'a for after salam, because sujud is the closest moment.

The du'a that gathers everything

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي، وَارْحَمْنِي، وَاهْدِنِي، وَعَافِنِي، وَارْزُقْنِي

"Ya Allah, ampunilah aku, rahmatilah aku, berilah aku petunjuk, sejahterakanlah aku, dan berilah aku rezeki."

Muslim 2697 (Tariq bin Asyam) · Sahih

When someone embraced Islam, the Prophet ﷺ would teach them solat, then this du'a.

WordingRequestMeaning
اغْفِرْ لِيKeampunanForgive my sins
وَارْحَمْنِيRahmatAllah's love and compassion
وَاهْدِنِيHidayahKeep me on the right path
وَعَافِنِي'AfiyahSafety from illness, calamity and harm
وَارْزُقْنِيRezekiHalal, good and blessed provision

In another narration the Prophet ﷺ said: "These gather for you the good of your dunia and your akhirat" (Muslim 2697).

Did you know? We already say this du'a every day! It's almost the same as the du'a between the two sujud: "Allahummaghfir li, warhamni, wa 'afini, wahdini, warzuqni" (Abu Dawud 850 · Ibn 'Abbas · hasan). From now on, say it with understanding.

② Munajat — talking to Allah

Solat is munajat: imagine you're speaking with Allah. "Apabila seseorang daripada kamu berdiri dalam solatnya, sesungguhnya dia sedang bermunajat dengan Tuhannya" (Bukhari 405 · Anas).

In a hadis qudsi, Allah says: "Aku membahagikan solat antara Aku dan hamba-Ku kepada dua bahagian, dan bagi hamba-Ku apa yang dia minta." When the servant says "Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqim", Allah says: "Ini untuk hamba-Ku, dan bagi hamba-Ku apa yang dia minta" (Muslim 395).

Every rakaat we ask for hidayah. But asking without making the effort to seek it — that's our test. The sahabat tasted khusyuk, sweetness and satisfaction standing before Allah.

③ Peace of soul (tuma'ninatun-nafs)

Real rest is in solat — not rest from solat.

يَا بِلَالُ أَقِمِ الصَّلَاةَ أَرِحْنَا بِهَا

"Wahai Bilal, iqamahkan solat, rehatkanlah kami dengannya."

Abu Dawud 4985 · Sahih (al-Albani)

7. Solat — a shield from sin

اتْلُ مَا أُوحِيَ إِلَيْكَ مِنَ الْكِتَابِ وَأَقِمِ الصَّلَاةَ ۖ إِنَّ الصَّلَاةَ تَنْهَىٰ عَنِ الْفَحْشَاءِ وَالْمُنكَرِ ۗ وَلَذِكْرُ اللَّهِ أَكْبَرُ

"Bacalah apa yang diwahyukan kepadamu daripada Al-Kitab dan dirikanlah solat. Sesungguhnya solat itu mencegah daripada perbuatan keji dan mungkar. Dan sesungguhnya mengingati Allah itu lebih besar."

Al-'Ankabut 29:45

  • اتْلُ (utlu) is a command — Allah Himself orders us to read and tadabbur the Quran. Tilawah linguistically also means to follow: read, then follow.
  • Wa ladhikrullahi akbar: the greatest form of zikir is solat, because it has all three kinds: heart (niat, khusyuk), tongue (recitation, tasbih, du'a), and body (standing, rukuk, sujud).

Ar-Ra'd 13:28 — only in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest. The Quran itself is a cure (syifa') and rahmat (Al-Isra' 17:82; Fussilat 41:44). And Allah says: "Aku bersama hamba-Ku apabila dia mengingati-Ku" (Bukhari 7405).

Umar r.a. — stabbed, yet the solat continued. Umar was stabbed just after saying takbir for Subuh. He pulled Abdurrahman bin 'Auf forward, and the congregation continued to the end (Bukhari 3700). Then he said: "Tiada bahagian dalam Islam bagi orang yang meninggalkan solat" — and prayed while his wound was still bleeding (Muwatta' Malik · athar, sahih isnad). Why? Because for them, solat was peace, medicine and cure — not a burden.

8. The Prophet's ﷺ du'a in distress

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ الْعَظِيمُ الْحَلِيمُ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ رَبُّ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَرَبُّ الْأَرْضِ وَرَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْكَرِيمِ

"Tiada Tuhan melainkan Allah, Yang Maha Agung lagi Maha Penyantun. Tiada Tuhan melainkan Allah, Tuhan Arasy yang agung. Tiada Tuhan melainkan Allah, Tuhan langit, Tuhan bumi dan Tuhan Arasy yang mulia."

Bukhari 6345 · Muslim 2730 (Ibn 'Abbas) · Sahih

What did the Prophet ﷺ give the sahabat? Solat · Al-Quran · Doa.

Reflection

  1. Do I read the Quran just to finish it — or to be guided and to change?
  2. When my heart is restless, do I return to remembering Allah and to the Quran?
  3. Is my recitation making me love Allah more, fear sin more, and try to be a better servant?

Summary

  1. Solat purifies the soul. The night prayer is the honour of a believer.
  2. In daily life, solat should be what makes us feel satisfied and redha, as it did for the Prophet ﷺ.
  3. Dunia is a flower. Real rezeki is iman, khusyuk, ilmu and amanah.
  4. Don't fear what Allah has guaranteed; fear not obeying. The goal is taqwa.
  5. Seek rezeki — but for obedience and infaq, not hoarding.
  6. Solat gives three things: closeness (sujud), munajat, and peace.
  7. Solat is the greatest zikir and a shield from sin.
