Class Notes · Tadabbur Siri 10

Surah An-Nas

Thursday, 13 August 2026  |  Surah ke-114 · 6 ayat  |  Protection from the whisper inside
In short
  • Seek refuge in Allah as Rabb, Malik and Ilah — from the whisper inside.
  • Al-Khannas retreats when we remember Allah.
  • Shaitan only invites; the choice — and responsibility — is ours.

2. Its place among Al-Mu'awwidhat

Al-Mu'awwidhat = Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas. Al-Falaq protects from evil that comes from outside; An-Nas protects from the evil whisper that gets inside us — into the heart.

Sunnah: the Prophet ﷺ recited them when ill, and taught us to read the 3 Qul three times in the morning and evening (Tirmidhi 3575 · hasan). The hadith says "morning and evening" in general — commonly done after Subuh and around Maghrib, but not tied to those two prayers.

3. Rabb, Malik, Ilah — three ayat, three attributes

AyatAttributeMeaning
1 — RabbCreator, Owner, ManagerWe seek protection from Allah alone — not a bomoh, jinn or any creature. The Prophet ﷺ himself sought protection only from Allah.
2 — MalikThe King who rulesHis power over everything is unopposed; no one can stop it.
3 — IlahThe One who must be worshippedNot just Creator and Ruler — we must ask why we do everything: for Allah, or for what? We look for Allah in hardship and forget Him in ease.
Hajar a.s.: full yakin in Allah while still running between Safa and Marwah seven times looking for water — then Allah brought out Zamzam. Tawakkal isn't just waiting; effort and yakin go together. (The story of Hajar & Ismail is in Sahih al-Bukhari.)
  • Ibn 'Abbas r.a.: "Apabila kamu meminta, mintalah kepada Allah. Apabila kamu memohon pertolongan, mohonlah kepada Allah." (Tirmidhi 2516 · hasan sahih)
  • Luqman 31:25 — ask them who created the heavens and earth and they'll say "Allah" — yet they still worshipped idols. Our fitrah already knows God is One.
  • Fatir 35:3 — is there any creator other than Allah who provides for you from the sky and earth? So how are you turned away?
  • Muslim 2999 — the believer's affairs are all good: in ease he is grateful, in hardship he is patient.

4. Al-Khannas — the whisper that retreats

Al-Khannas = one who retreats, hides, disappears. When a servant remembers Allah and obeys Him, the shaitan's whisper weakens and backs off.

Zikrullah isn't only saying Allah's name — it covers the heart (remembering and depending on Him), the tongue (zikir, du'a, good speech), and actions (obeying, avoiding what He forbids).

وَمَن يَعْشُ عَن ذِكْرِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ نُقَيِّضْ لَهُ شَيْطَانًا فَهُوَ لَهُ قَرِينٌ

"Dan sesiapa yang berpaling daripada mengingati (Allah) Ar-Rahman, Kami adakan baginya syaitan, lalu syaitan itu menjadi temannya."

Az-Zukhruf 43:36

5. The whisper in the chest — the heart is the control centre

Sudur (chests) here means the heart. Shaitan has no power to force — he only invites and whispers. That's why the heart must be guarded: a small whisper can lead to a big deed.

"Ketahuilah, sesungguhnya dalam jasad ada seketul daging. Jika ia baik, baiklah seluruh jasad; jika ia rosak, rosaklah seluruh jasad. Ketahuilah, itulah hati."

Bukhari 52; Muslim 1599 (An-Nu'man bin Basyir) · Sahih

Ibrahim 14:22 — on the Day of Judgement shaitan will say: "Aku tidak mempunyai kuasa terhadap kamu kecuali aku mengajak kamu, lalu kamu menyahut ajakanku. Maka janganlah kamu mencela aku, tetapi celalah diri kamu sendiri."

Shaitan invites and whispers — but we remain responsible for our choices. Don't just blame shaitan. (Noted in class: things like music can also distract the heart from remembering Allah.)

6. From jinn and from people

Evil whispers can come from jinn/shaitan — and from people too, who can also whisper and invite to evil. So we seek protection from both.

Summary

  1. Return to Allah as Rabb, Malik, Ilah.
  2. Seek protection from the whisperer who hides.
  3. The whisper enters the heart — so guard the heart.
  4. Increase zikrullah so Al-Khannas retreats.
  5. Don't blame shaitan alone — the choice is ours.
  6. Whispers come from both jinn and people.

Reflections

  • Temptation rarely shouts — it whispers. Like shaitan, pressure to compromise usually comes as a small, reasonable-sounding suggestion ("it's fine, no one will know"). Guarding the heart means catching it at the whisper stage.
  • We can be someone else's whisperer. Ayat 6 includes people — advice given for our own benefit rather than theirs is exactly that.
