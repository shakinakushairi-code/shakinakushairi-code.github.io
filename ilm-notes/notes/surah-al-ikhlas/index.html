Surah Al-Ikhlas

Thursday, 6 August 2026  |  Surah ke-112 · 4 ayat · Makkiyyah (majority view)  |  Who Allah is
In short
  • Four ayat on who Allah is: One, self-sufficient, unborn, without equal.
  • Correct tauhid produces ikhlas — deeds for Allah alone.
  • Big deeds done for show can be the first thrown into the Fire.

Most scholars hold it's Makkiyyah — the stronger view, based on its asbab an-nuzul: the musyrikin of Makkah asked the Prophet ﷺ to describe his Lord.

2. Why is it called Al-Ikhlas?

Its core is tauhid — it explains who Allah is, not what He gives. And correct tauhid produces ikhlas: doing deeds for Allah — not for people, not for praise, not for a name, not to be known.

"Kalau Allah tahu, perlu ke orang lain tahu?"

3. Equal to a third of the Quran — what does it mean?

The Prophet ﷺ asked: "Tidak mampukah seorang daripada kamu membaca sepertiga Al-Quran dalam satu malam?" — and said Al-Ikhlas is equal to a third of the Quran (Bukhari 5015, 6643; Muslim 811).

It equals a third in reward — it doesn't replace reading a third. If someone made a nazar to read a third of the Quran, reading Al-Ikhlas three times doesn't fulfil it. Same reward, not the same function.

Loving this surah: an imam at Masjid Quba' recited it in every rakaat. When asked why, he said he loved it because it describes Ar-Rahman. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Kecintaanmu terhadap surah itu akan memasukkanmu ke dalam syurga." (Bukhari 774, Anas)

4. Four attributes in four ayat

AyatSifatYang dinafikan
1 — AhadEsa — one, alone, no partnerShirk in His essence
2 — As-SamadPerfect; everything depends on Him, and He depends on no oneAny deficiency or need
3 — Lam yalid wa lam yuladAzali (no beginning) & Abadi (no end)Lineage, descent, a beginning
4 — Lam yakun lahu kufuwan ahadNo rival, partner, spouse or equalLikening Him to creation (tasybih)

Ayat 3 rejects three dangerous claims

All three challenged the prophets and their dakwah:

  • Nasrani: Isa a.s. is the son of God.
  • Some of the Yahudi: Uzair is the son of Allah — At-Taubah 9:30.
  • The Arab musyrikin: the angels are Allah's daughters.

Azali & abadi: every creation has a beginning and an end; Allah has neither. He was not created and will never perish — anything that is born must have a beginning.

Ash-Shura 42:11 reinforces ayat 4

لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِ شَيْءٌ ۖ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْبَصِيرُ

"Tiada sesuatu pun yang serupa dengan-Nya, dan Dialah Yang Maha Mendengar lagi Maha Melihat."

Ash-Shura 42:11

Allah created pairs for humans and animals — but nothing resembles Him. He creates; He does not resemble what He creates.

5. The first three thrown into Hell

Muslim 1905 (Abu Hurairah) · Sahih

  1. The reciter / student of knowledge — learned and taught the Quran so people would say "he's an 'alim / a qari'". He wanted to be praised.
  2. The fighter — fought so people would say "he's a hero". He wanted to be known.
  3. The one who gave sedekah — gave a lot so people would say "he's generous". He wanted people to see and praise him.
Deeds that look big to people aren't necessarily big to Allah. Without the right niat, a deed can even become a cause of destruction.

فَاعْبُدِ اللَّهَ مُخْلِصًا لَّهُ الدِّينَ

"Maka sembahlah Allah dengan mengikhlaskan agama kepada-Nya."

Az-Zumar 39:2

6. Amalan: 3 Qul, morning and evening

The Prophet ﷺ told Abdullah bin Khubaib r.a. to read Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq and An-Nas three times in the morning and evening — "it will suffice you against everything" (Tirmidhi 3575; Abu Dawud 5082 · hasan; an-Nawawi: sahih isnad).

Other ayat from class

  • Yunus 10:7–8 — those content with dunia and heedless of Allah's signs: their abode is the Fire.
  • Yasin 36:78–79 — "Who will give life to bones that have crumbled?" — the One who created them the first time.
  • Hadis qudsi — what Allah has prepared for the righteous: what no eye has seen, no ear has heard, and no heart has imagined (Bukhari 3244; Muslim 2824).

Reflections

  • As-Samad: only One is truly dependable. Everything else we rely on — jobs, savings, people, insurance — is created, and anything created can fail. Plan as well as we can, but don't treat any plan as samad.
  • Amount doesn't protect niat. Muslim 1905 shows a big donation given for recognition can be a cause of ruin, while a small one given secretly is accepted.
  • Same reward ≠ same function. Al-Ikhlas three times equals a third of the Quran in reward, but doesn't replace a vow to read it. "Equivalent value, so it can substitute" is a mistake in many areas of life.
