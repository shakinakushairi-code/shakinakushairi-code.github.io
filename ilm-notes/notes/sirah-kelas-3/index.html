Tarbiyah 'Aqliyyah: Menyucikan Akal

Tuesday, 27 October 2026  |  Ustaz Abd Muein  |  Fasa Makkah Awal  |  Kitab: As-Sirah an-Nabawiyyah, al-Sallabi, Bab 2, m/s 165
In short
  • The Prophet ﷺ trained the sahabat's minds, not just their hearts.
  • Look at the sky and your food until you feel Allah's greatness.
  • The mind is an amanah: follow dalil, and tabayyun before you share.
"Sesungguhnya umat-umat itu kekal selagi akhlaknya kekal. Jika akhlak mereka hilang, maka hilanglah mereka." — a line of poetry by Ahmad Syauqi (d. 1932), the "Prince of Poets". Good hikmah, but not a hadith or dalil.

1. Recap: purifying the heart

In earlier classes, the Prophet ﷺ purified the sahabat's hearts through zikir, du'a, tilawah Al-Quran and qiyam (night prayer). This class moves to the second process: purifying the mind.

2. Tarbiyah 'aqliyyah — how the Prophet ﷺ educated the mind

  • His education of the sahabat covered every aspect — spiritual, physical, intellectual, administrative — all based on the Quran.
  • The Quran came to fix mankind as a whole: roh, jasad and akal.

The process: Memerhati → Menghayati → Tafakkur → Tadabbur → Memikul tanggungjawab dakwah.

Dakwah is the goal. A trained mind isn't for showing off knowledge — it's for passing it on.

3. Allah's signs only benefit those who believe

قُلِ انظُرُوا مَاذَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ ۚ وَمَا تُغْنِي الْآيَاتُ وَالنُّذُرُ عَن قَوْمٍ لَّا يُؤْمِنُونَ

"Katakanlah: Perhatikanlah apa yang ada di langit dan di bumi. Namun tanda-tanda dan peringatan tidak berguna bagi kaum yang tidak beriman."

Yunus 10:101

Show any sign you like — if the heart isn't touched, they still won't believe. Only the believer reflects and grows in iman when seeing Allah's greatness.

4. Al-Mulk — look at the sky, again and again

الَّذِي خَلَقَ سَبْعَ سَمَاوَاتٍ طِبَاقًا ۖ مَّا تَرَىٰ فِي خَلْقِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ مِن تَفَاوُتٍ ۖ فَارْجِعِ الْبَصَرَ هَلْ تَرَىٰ مِن فُطُورٍ ۝ ثُمَّ ارْجِعِ الْبَصَرَ كَرَّتَيْنِ يَنقَلِبْ إِلَيْكَ الْبَصَرُ خَاسِئًا وَهُوَ حَسِيرٌ

"Dialah yang menciptakan tujuh langit berlapis-lapis. Kamu tidak akan melihat sebarang ketidakseimbangan pada ciptaan Ar-Rahman. Maka pandanglah sekali lagi, adakah kamu melihat sebarang retakan? Kemudian pandanglah berulang kali, nescaya pandanganmu akan kembali kepadamu dalam keadaan hina dan letih."

Al-Mulk 67:3–4

Allah tells us to look at the sky over and over — searching for a flaw — so that we feel how great Allah is.

Link to solat: once you've felt Allah's greatness, when the time for solat comes, stop and pray. The Most Great is the One calling you.

5. Ulul Albab — people who use their minds

Ulul Albab = people of understanding (lubb = the pure core of the mind). The Quran defines them itself:

الَّذِينَ يَذْكُرُونَ اللَّهَ قِيَامًا وَقُعُودًا وَعَلَىٰ جُنُوبِهِمْ وَيَتَفَكَّرُونَ فِي خَلْقِ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ

"(Iaitu) orang-orang yang mengingati Allah ketika berdiri, duduk dan berbaring, dan mereka memikirkan tentang penciptaan langit dan bumi…"

Ali 'Imran 3:191

Ciri Ulul AlbabFungsi
Zikir — in every stateHati
Tafakkur — reflecting on creationAkal

Ulul Albab aren't just clever. They combine a heart that remembers with a mind that thinks.

6. Tadabbur & tazakkur — what the mind is for

The mind is given for tadabbur and tazakkur: tadabbur → take the reminder → be careful.

كِتَابٌ أَنزَلْنَاهُ إِلَيْكَ مُبَارَكٌ لِّيَدَّبَّرُوا آيَاتِهِ وَلِيَتَذَكَّرَ أُولُو الْأَلْبَابِ

"Kitab yang Kami turunkan kepadamu, penuh keberkatan, supaya mereka mentadabbur ayat-ayatnya dan supaya Ulul Albab mengambil peringatan."

Sad 38:29

7. 'Abasa 80:24–32 — look at your food

فَلْيَنظُرِ الْإِنسَانُ إِلَىٰ طَعَامِهِ ۝ أَنَّا صَبَبْنَا الْمَاءَ صَبًّا

"Maka hendaklah manusia memerhatikan makanannya. Sesungguhnya Kami telah mencurahkan air (hujan) dengan curahan yang banyak."

'Abasa 80:24–25

Allah tells us to think about the food in front of us. He starts with the rain (25), then the earth split open (26), grains, grapes, vegetables, olives, dates, lush gardens, fruit and pasture (27–31) — as provision for you and your livestock (32).

Critical thinking: a plate of rice passes through a long chain — rain, soil, seed, sun, farmer, transport. We don't fully control a single link. The ayat trains us to follow the chain of causes all the way back to the Causer.

8. The mind as the basis of responsibility

The mind is one of the abilities Allah gives us — and it's the basis of taklif (being held responsible, mukallaf).

رُفِعَ الْقَلَمُ عَنْ ثَلَاثَةٍ: عَنِ النَّائِمِ حَتَّى يَسْتَيْقِظَ، وَعَنِ الصَّبِيِّ حَتَّى يَحْتَلِمَ، وَعَنِ الْمَجْنُونِ حَتَّى يَعْقِلَ

"Pena (catatan amal) diangkat daripada tiga golongan: orang yang tidur hingga dia bangun, kanak-kanak hingga dia baligh, dan orang gila hingga dia berakal."

Abu Dawud 4403 ('Ali r.a.) · Sahih (al-Albani)

No mind, no taklif. The mind is an amanah — and it will be questioned.

9. Al-Isra' 17:36 — don't follow without knowledge

وَلَا تَقْفُ مَا لَيْسَ لَكَ بِهِ عِلْمٌ ۚ إِنَّ السَّمْعَ وَالْبَصَرَ وَالْفُؤَادَ كُلُّ أُولَٰئِكَ كَانَ عَنْهُ مَسْئُولًا

"Dan janganlah kamu mengikuti sesuatu yang kamu tidak mempunyai pengetahuan tentangnya. Sesungguhnya pendengaran, penglihatan dan hati, semuanya akan dipertanggungjawabkan."

Al-Isra' 17:36

Don't do or follow anything that has no basis, knowledge or dalil. Hearing, sight and heart — the tools of the mind — will all be asked about.

10. Al-Hujurat 49:6 — tabayyun before you share

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِن جَاءَكُمْ فَاسِقٌ بِنَبَإٍ فَتَبَيَّنُوا أَن تُصِيبُوا قَوْمًا بِجَهَالَةٍ فَتُصْبِحُوا عَلَىٰ مَا فَعَلْتُمْ نَادِمِينَ

"Wahai orang-orang yang beriman, jika datang kepada kamu seorang fasiq membawa sesuatu berita, maka selidikilah kebenarannya, supaya kamu tidak menimpakan musibah kepada sesuatu kaum tanpa mengetahui keadaannya, lalu kamu menyesal atas apa yang telah kamu lakukan."

Al-Hujurat 49:6

Adab and akhlak: get some news — don't share it straight away. Check first whether it's true.

Tabayyun in the WhatsApp era. Before forwarding, ask three questions: (1) Who's the source? Trustworthy or not? (2) Is there proof? The original reference, not "dengar kata". (3) What's the damage if it's wrong — who'll be wronged? Same principle for hadith: that's why we check sunnah.com and dorar.net before sharing.

11. Da'watul 'aql — the Prophet ﷺ invited them to think

The Prophet ﷺ invited the sahabat to think about Allah's creation. Islam doesn't ask for blind faith, but faith born from observation and reflection.

12. Al-Hijr 15:85 — nothing was created in vain

وَمَا خَلَقْنَا السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا إِلَّا بِالْحَقِّ

"Dan tidaklah Kami ciptakan langit dan bumi serta apa yang ada di antara keduanya melainkan dengan kebenaran."

Al-Hijr 15:85

"With truth" means Allah didn't create in vain — there's always a reason and purpose. That includes us: our lives have a purpose too.

13. Du'a when looking at the sky

رَبَّنَا مَا خَلَقْتَ هَٰذَا بَاطِلًا سُبْحَانَكَ فَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

"Wahai Tuhan kami, tidaklah Engkau menciptakan semua ini dengan sia-sia. Maha Suci Engkau, maka peliharalah kami daripada azab neraka."

Ali 'Imran 3:191

Its basis in the sunnah: Ibn 'Abbas r.a. stayed the night at his aunt Maimunah r.a.'s house. In the last third of the night, the Prophet ﷺ woke, looked up at the sky, recited Ali 'Imran 3:190 to the end of the surah, then made wudhu', used the siwak and prayed (Bukhari 4569; Muslim 256). What's narrated is reciting 3:190 to the end of the surah when waking at night — an-Nawawi lists it as sunnah then (al-Adhkar). Reading it whenever we look at the sky is a good practice based on the ayat's meaning.

Renungan

  1. When did I last look at the sky — or the food in front of me — and really think about its Creator?
  2. Do I use my mind to submit to Allah, or to find excuses?
  3. How much news did I share this week without tabayyun?
  4. What's the "truth" — the purpose — behind my own life?

Rumusan

  1. The Prophet ﷺ educated the sahabat completely — roh, jasad and akal — all based on the Quran.
  2. Tarbiyah of the mind: observe → absorb → tafakkur → tadabbur → dakwah.
  3. Allah's signs only benefit a believing heart (10:101).
  4. Look at the sky and your food, feel Allah's greatness, then answer the call to solat.
  5. Ulul Albab = zikir (heart) + tafakkur (mind).
  6. The mind is the basis of taklif and will be questioned (17:36).
  7. Tabayyun is the adab of the mind: check before you share (49:6).
  8. Nothing was created in vain (15:85, 3:191).
