Surah Al-Fatihah: Ayat 2

Al-hamdu lillahi Rabbil-'alamin  |  Praise, syukur, and who our Rabb really is
In short
  • All praise is Allah's — He doesn't need it; it trains our hearts.
  • Syukur has three levels: heart, tongue, and using the nikmat in obedience.
  • Rabb = Creator, Owner and Manager of everything.

2. Why does the surah open with praise?

  1. Allah is the Creator, Owner and Manager of the whole universe.
  2. Allah is teaching us how to praise Him properly.
  3. Allah is Al-Ghaniy — He doesn't need our praise. Our praise adds nothing to His kingdom; our criticism takes nothing from His power.
So the praise in our solat isn't for Allah's benefit — it's to train our hearts.

Excessive praise of people is forbidden

Al-Miqdad bin al-Aswad r.a. narrated that the Prophet ﷺ told us to throw dust in the faces of those who over-praise (Muslim 3002) — a strong rebuke of praise that can ruin the person being praised.

The danger of ujub and kibr

لَا يَدْخُلُ الْجَنَّةَ مَنْ كَانَ فِي قَلْبِهِ مِثْقَالُ ذَرَّةٍ مِنْ كِبْرٍ

"Tidak akan masuk syurga orang yang di dalam hatinya terdapat sebesar zarah daripada kesombongan."

Muslim 91 · Sahih

Zarah = something tiny, like the fine dust you see floating when sunlight comes through a gap in the window.

3. The three levels of syukur

PeringkatMaksud
1Syukur dengan hatiFully acknowledging that every nikmat comes from Allah
2Syukur dengan lisanMentioning and praising Allah for it — Ad-Dhuha 93:11
3Syukur dengan anggota badanUsing the nikmat to obey Him
Level 3 in action: the Prophet ﷺ prayed at night until his feet swelled. Aisyah r.a. asked why, when his sins were already forgiven. He said: "Tidakkah aku mahu menjadi hamba yang bersyukur?" (Bukhari 4837; Muslim 2819)

Syukur by the tongue, from Nabi Sulaiman a.s.: "Ini adalah daripada kurniaan Tuhanku" — An-Naml 27:40.

4. When do we praise Allah?

In every state. The Prophet ﷺ had a pair of phrases:

KeadaanSebut
Something you like / a nikmatالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي بِنِعْمَتِهِ تَتِمُّ الصَّالِحَاتُ
"Segala puji bagi Allah yang dengan nikmat-Nya sempurnalah segala kebaikan."
Something you dislikeالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ حَالٍ
"Segala puji bagi Allah dalam setiap keadaan."

Ibn Majah 3803 (Aisyah) · Hasan — graded differently by scholars, strengthened by supporting chains (al-Hakim, al-Albani)

Note: this is a pair, not one du'a — many infographics only show half. For a real musibah, the stronger dalil is innā lillāhi wa innā ilaihi rāji'ūn (Muslim 918).

فَاذْكُرُونِي أَذْكُرْكُمْ وَاشْكُرُوا لِي وَلَا تَكْفُرُونِ

"Maka ingatlah kamu kepada-Ku, nescaya Aku ingat kepada kamu; dan bersyukurlah kepada-Ku, dan janganlah kamu kufur."

Al-Baqarah 2:152

  • "Ingat Allah" = always aware Allah is there — zikir, du'a, solat, doing good for His sake.
  • "Allah ingat kamu" = He gives His attention, rahmat, help and good reward.
  • "Bersyukur" = valuing His nikmat — not just saying Alhamdulillah, but using it for the right things.
  • "Jangan kufur" = don't pretend not to see the nikmat — forgetting Allah, endless complaining, using it for sin, or thinking every success comes from yourself.

Why is it hard to praise Allah in hardship?

Because we see musibah with a narrow view and don't know the hikmah behind it. Nabi Khidir a.s. damaged a boat (Al-Kahf 18:79) — what looked like a disaster was actually what saved it from a king who seized every good boat.

عَجَبًا لِأَمْرِ الْمُؤْمِنِ، إِنَّ أَمْرَهُ كُلَّهُ خَيْرٌ

"Menakjubkan urusan orang beriman — semua urusannya baik baginya." Senang → bersyukur → baik baginya. Susah → bersabar → baik baginya.

Muslim 2999 (Suhaib) · Sahih

Its pair of warnings — Ibrahim 14:7: be grateful and I'll increase you; be ungrateful and My punishment is severe.

5. What does "Rabb" mean?

NamaMakna
Al-Khaliq (الخالق)Creator
Al-Malik (المالك)Owner
Al-Mudabbir (المدبر)Manager and Administrator of all the worlds

No one else creates, owns and runs the universe. All three meet only in Him — this is the argument of tauhid rububiyyah.

6. What does "Al-'Alamin" mean?

From الْعَالَم — "something that serves as a sign". Every creation is a sign pointing to Allah's greatness, power and perfection.

LookDalilIsi
Up — the skyQaf 50:6The sky built without a single crack
Down — a mosquitoAl-Baqarah 2:26Allah isn't shy to give the example of a mosquito, or something smaller

Even a mosquito: a tiny needle, finds you in the dark, detects the right blood, we're scared of it despite its size, and it can carry diseases that kill.

Allah's greatness shows in the vast and in the tiny — both are signs leading us to know Rabbil-'alamin.

Reflections

  • Syukur has three levels, and most of us stop at two. We acknowledge it in the heart and say Alhamdulillah — but level three, actually using the nikmat in obedience, is the one that takes effort and real decisions.
  • If Allah is Al-Malik, we don't truly own anything. What we call "mine" is held on trust, with conditions.
  • Muslim 2999 moves the measure from outcome to response. Gain or loss can both be good for a believer — what's judged is our sabar and syukur, not the numbers.
