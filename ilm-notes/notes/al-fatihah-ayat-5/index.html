Surah Al-Fatihah: Ayat 5

Thursday, 25 June 2026  |  Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka nasta'in  |  The turning point of Al-Fatihah
In short
  • "Only You we worship, only You we ask" — obey first, then ask.
  • Ibadah is everything Allah loves, including small kindnesses.
  • Worship is incomplete if we harm people; the best deed is the most sincere.

1. Teks & maksud

2. The pivot of Al-Fatihah

Ayat 1–4 are praise of Allah; ayat 5–7 are the servant's side of the covenant. The surah is divided between Allah and His servant — and this ayat is the border (hadis qudsi, Muslim 395).

Two grammar points that carry 'aqidah

  1. Putting the object first (iyyaka) gives exclusivity (hasr): "only You" — not just "we worship You". It rules out shirk in worship and in reliance.
  2. The deliberate order: na'budu before nasta'in — worship first, then asking for help. We give (obey) before we ask.

3. What is ibadah?

الْعِبَادَةُ اسْمٌ جَامِعٌ لِكُلِّ مَا يُحِبُّهُ اللَّهُ وَيَرْضَاهُ مِنَ الْأَقْوَالِ وَالْأَعْمَالِ الظَّاهِرَةِ وَالْبَاطِنَةِ

"Ibadah ialah nama yang merangkumi segala yang Allah cintai dan redhai, sama ada perkataan atau perbuatan, zahir dan batin."

Ibn Taymiyyah, Majmu' al-Fatawa 10/149

Ibadah isn't only solat. Anything done for Allah and in line with the sunnah counts: solat, puasa, zakat, seeking knowledge, being good to parents, helping people, smiling at your brother, easing someone's hardship.

4. The deen rests on two relationships

حَبْلٌ مِنَ اللَّهِ — with Allahحَبْلٌ مِنَ النَّاسِ — with people
Solat · puasa · zikir · ikhlas · tawakkalGood to parents · caring for neighbours · helping those in need · keeping amanah · spreading rahmah

Worship of Allah isn't complete if people's rights are trampled.

The Prophet ﷺ was told of a woman known for much night prayer, fasting and sedekah, but who hurt her neighbours with her tongue. He said: "Dia di dalam neraka." Then of a woman who did only the obligatory prayers and gave small sedekah, but didn't hurt her neighbours. He said: "Dia di dalam syurga."

al-Adab al-Mufrad 119 (Abu Hurairah) · Sahih (al-Albani)

5. The best deed: sincere and following the sunnah

الَّذِي خَلَقَ الْمَوْتَ وَالْحَيَاةَ لِيَبْلُوَكُمْ أَيُّكُمْ أَحْسَنُ عَمَلًا

"(Dialah) yang menciptakan mati dan hidup untuk menguji kamu, siapakah antara kamu yang paling baik amalannya."

Al-Mulk 67:2

The salaf understood ahsanu 'amala as the most sincere and most in line with the sunnah — not the most in quantity. The ayat says ahsan (best), not akthar (most).

From class: be wary of viral "read this X times to get Y" content with no dalil. The authenticated daily sunnah is already more than anyone can do in a day — there's no need to invent new formulas.

6. The deeds most beloved to Allah

أَحَبُّ النَّاسِ إِلَى اللَّهِ أَنْفَعُهُمْ لِلنَّاسِ

"Manusia yang paling dicintai Allah ialah yang paling bermanfaat kepada manusia. Dan amalan yang paling dicintai Allah ialah kegembiraan yang engkau masukkan ke dalam hati seorang Muslim, atau engkau hilangkan kesusahannya, atau engkau lunaskan hutangnya, atau engkau hilangkan laparnya."

At-Tabarani, al-Awsat · Hasan/sahih (al-Albani: sahih in al-Silsilah al-Sahihah 906; hasan li-ghayrihi in Sahih al-Targhib 2623)

Abu Bakr r.a.

  • He'll be called from every gate of Jannah because of his many good deeds (Bukhari 1897; Muslim 1027).
  • Al-Lail 92:17–18 — "the most pious, who gives his wealth to purify himself": many mufassirin say this refers to Abu Bakr, who spent his wealth freeing slaves tortured for Islam.
  • From class: Abu Bakr is remembered not for qiyam or khatam, but for his infaq — worship through wealth can be what defines you before Allah.

7. Why we were created

وَمَا خَلَقْتُ الْجِنَّ وَالْإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُونِ ۝ مَا أُرِيدُ مِنْهُم مِّن رِّزْقٍ وَمَا أُرِيدُ أَن يُطْعِمُونِ ۝ إِنَّ اللَّهَ هُوَ الرَّزَّاقُ ذُو الْقُوَّةِ الْمَتِينُ

"Dan tidak Aku ciptakan jin dan manusia melainkan untuk menyembah-Ku. Aku tidak menghendaki rezeki daripada mereka, dan Aku tidak menghendaki mereka memberi-Ku makan. Sesungguhnya Allah, Dialah Pemberi rezeki, Yang mempunyai kekuatan lagi Maha Kukuh."

Adh-Dhariyat 51:56–58

  1. Rezeki comes from Allah.
  2. The means must still be pursued, while the heart depends on Allah.
  3. Tawakkal doesn't cancel effort.

Reflections

  • Give before you ask. Na'budu comes before nasta'in. We often come to Allah with a list of requests before any commitment of obedience — the ayat reverses that order.
  • Ibadah is wide. If ibadah is everything Allah loves, then our daily work, done honestly and for His sake, is worship too.
  • Two ropes, both needed. Strong with Allah but harsh with people — or kind to people but careless with Allah — both fall short. The hadith of the two women is the sharpest warning.
  • Allah needs nothing from us (51:57). Every sedekah and act of help is for people's benefit — so there's no room to feel we've done Him a favour.
