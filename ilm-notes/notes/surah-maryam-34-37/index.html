Part A — The virtues of solat

This continues the theme of "solat and zakat" in 19:31.

Three traits of the muttaqin — Al-Baqarah 2:2–3

الَّذِينَ يُؤْمِنُونَ بِالْغَيْبِ وَيُقِيمُونَ الصَّلَاةَ وَمِمَّا رَزَقْنَاهُمْ يُنفِقُونَ

"(Iaitu) orang-orang yang beriman kepada yang ghaib, mendirikan solat, dan membelanjakan sebahagian rezeki yang Kami berikan kepada mereka."

Al-Baqarah 2:3

Believing in the unseen · establishing solat · spending from what Allah gave (zakat and sedekah).

Solat is zikir

Forms of zikir: (1) solat, (2) the Quran, (3) tasbih and tahmid, (4) du'a. The Quran as zikir: read → listen → understand — and understanding has levels: translation → tafsir → action → reflection. That's tadabbur.

إِنَّنِي أَنَا اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنَا فَاعْبُدْنِي وَأَقِمِ الصَّلَاةَ لِذِكْرِي

"Sesungguhnya Akulah Allah; tiada tuhan melainkan Aku; maka sembahlah Aku dan dirikanlah solat untuk mengingati-Ku."

Ta-Ha 20:14

Al-'Ankabut 29:45 — "sesungguhnya solat mencegah daripada perbuatan keji dan mungkar; dan sesungguhnya mengingati Allah adalah lebih besar."

Light, help and du'a

  • "Bersuci itu separuh iman… solat itu cahaya, sedekah itu bukti, sabar itu sinar…" (Muslim 223, Abu Malik al-Asy'ari)
  • Al-Baqarah 2:153 — "Wahai orang beriman, mintalah pertolongan dengan sabar dan solat; sesungguhnya Allah bersama orang yang sabar."
  • "Saat paling hampir seorang hamba dengan Tuhannya ialah ketika dia sujud, maka perbanyakkanlah doa." (Muslim 482)
  • New Muslims were taught solat first, then this du'a: اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَارْحَمْنِي وَاهْدِنِي وَعَافِنِي وَارْزُقْنِي (Muslim 2697)

A way out, and rezeki

وَمَن يَتَّقِ اللَّهَ يَجْعَل لَّهُ مَخْرَجًا ۝ وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ

"Sesiapa yang bertakwa kepada Allah, Dia akan jadikan baginya jalan keluar, dan memberinya rezeki dari arah yang tidak disangka. Sesiapa yang bertawakkal kepada Allah, Allah mencukupkannya."

At-Talaq 65:2–3

The ayat links the way out and rezeki to taqwa and tawakkal; solat is the foremost trait of taqwa (2:3), so the link holds as explanation. The ayat that ties solat and rezeki directly is Ta-Ha 20:132: "Perintahkanlah keluargamu mendirikan solat dan bersabarlah menunaikannya. Kami tidak meminta rezeki daripadamu; Kamilah yang memberimu rezeki."

A good life — An-Nahl 16:97

مَنْ عَمِلَ صَالِحًا مِّن ذَكَرٍ أَوْ أُنثَىٰ وَهُوَ مُؤْمِنٌ فَلَنُحْيِيَنَّهُ حَيَاةً طَيِّبَةً

"Sesiapa yang beramal soleh, lelaki atau perempuan, sedang dia beriman, Kami akan hidupkan dia dengan kehidupan yang baik."

An-Nahl 16:97

Ibn Kathir's preferred view: it includes all of these. Its proof: "Sungguh beruntung orang yang memeluk Islam, diberi rezeki yang mencukupi, dan Allah jadikan dia berasa cukup dengan apa yang diberikan kepadanya." (Muslim 1054)

Solat strengthens the soul — Al-Ma'arij 70:19–23

Man was created anxious: panicking when hardship touches him, stingy when good touches him — except those who pray, who are constant in their prayer.

Coolness of the eyes, and rest

  • "Dijadikan aku menyukai daripada dunia: wanita dan wangian, dan dijadikan penyejuk mataku dalam solat." (Musnad Ahmad 12293; an-Nasa'i 3939 · hasan sahih) — note the popular "three things from your world" version isn't authentic: solat isn't part of dunia.
  • "Bangunlah wahai Bilal, iqamahkan solat, rehatkanlah kami dengannya." (Abu Dawud 4985–4986 · sahih)

Maryam 19:34–37

19:34 — the truth about Isa, still disputed

Everything Nabi Isa brought was truth — yet groups still argued against it.

An-Nisa' 4:171 itself: the Messiah Isa son of Maryam is only a messenger of Allah and His word conveyed to Maryam… don't say "three"… glory be to Him above having a son.

19:35 — two points

  1. It's not befitting for Allah to have a child.
  2. Kun fayakun — the answer to how Maryam conceived without a husband: Allah says "Be!"

MusyrikinThe angels are Allah's daughtersAn-Nahl 16:57

An argument from reason (from class): nobody argues that Adam is the son of God — yet he was created with neither father nor mother. "Perbandingan Isa di sisi Allah sama seperti Adam; Dia menciptanya daripada tanah, lalu berfirman: Jadilah! Maka jadilah ia" (Ali 'Imran 3:59). And if Allah had a child: He'd depend on creation, share a "divine DNA", and that child would die. It makes no sense.

19:36 — Isa's last words from the cradle

"Allah is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him." Isa repeats it in Al-Ma'idah 5:72. And on the Day of Judgement (Al-Ma'idah 5:116–117) Allah will ask him: "Did you tell people to take you and your mother as gods besides Allah?" He'll answer: "Glory be to You! It's not for me to say what I have no right to… I only told them what You commanded: worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord."

Reflections

  • Panic and stinginess have one cure. 70:19–21 describes two reactions — panicking in hardship, holding tight in ease. The ayat puts the cure in a daily discipline (da'imun, constant prayer), not one big decision.
  • Solat and giving belong together (2:3). Spending from what Allah gave flows from the connection solat builds.
  • What's "enough"? Ibn Kathir's view of the good life combines halal rezeki and contentment; Muslim 1054 says success is sufficiency plus being content with it — not the amount.
  • Tawakkal isn't the absence of planning (65:3). Like Maryam shaking the palm, we make the effort, then leave the outcome to Allah.
