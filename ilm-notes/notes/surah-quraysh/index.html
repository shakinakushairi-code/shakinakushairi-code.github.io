2. Who are the Quraysh?

The tribe of the Prophet ﷺ. Many prophets came from Bani Israil; Nabi Muhammad ﷺ came from the line of Nabi Ismail through Quraysh.

"Sesungguhnya Allah telah memilih Kinanah daripada keturunan Ismail, memilih Quraysh daripada Kinanah, memilih Bani Hasyim daripada Quraysh, dan memilihku daripada Bani Hasyim."

Muslim 2276 (Wathilah bin al-Asqa') · Sahih

3. Ayat 1–2 — the two trade journeys

MusimKeBarangan
Musim sejukYamanRempah, kain, permaidani
Musim panasSyamHasil pertanian, sayur, buah
  • The routes were full of robbers and bandits — yet Quraysh caravans weren't touched, because they were known as the guardians of the House of Allah.
  • After Allah Himself destroyed the army of the elephant (Surah Al-Fil), Quraysh's standing rose even higher.
  • At-tashwiq — ayat 1 opens with li- ("kerana…") without saying straight away what it's linked to. A style that makes the reader curious.
  • The trade details are historical information, not from the nas.

What is li- linked to? Ulama differ: (a) to Surah Al-Fil — Allah destroyed the army for the sake of Quraysh's security (Ibn Kathir, from Ibn Ishaq); (b) to ayat 3 — "because of this nikmat, worship the Lord of this House"; (c) an expression of wonder — "marvel at the habit of Quraysh" (chosen by Ibn Jarir at-Tabari). All valid; the ustaz's at-tashwiq explanation fits as a point of balaghah.

4. Rezeki in a barren valley

Makkah's land had nothing — yet Quraysh's rezeki overflowed, only because of the Kaabah.

إِنَّ اللَّهَ هُوَ الرَّزَّاقُ ذُو الْقُوَّةِ الْمَتِينُ

"Sesungguhnya Allah, Dialah Pemberi rezeki, Yang mempunyai kekuatan lagi Maha Kukuh."

Adh-Dhariyat 51:58

Why did Allah protect Makkah? In class: to protect the birthplace of the Prophet ﷺ, born in the Year of the Elephant. The clearest reason from the nas is the honour of the Kaabah as Baitullah (106:3, 29:67) — the two don't contradict.

Al-'Ankabut 29:67 — "Tidakkah mereka melihat bahawa Kami menjadikan (Makkah) tanah haram yang aman, sedangkan manusia di sekeliling mereka diculik dan dirampas?"

5. Ayat 3–4 — so worship Him

Allah has given the nikmat — so worship Him. Why do we still put nafsu and dunia first? Allah mentions two nikmat, food and safety, because they're the foundation of life.

فَمَن تَبِعَ هُدَايَ فَلَا خَوْفٌ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحْزَنُونَ

"Sesiapa yang mengikut petunjuk-Ku, nescaya tiada ketakutan atas mereka dan mereka tidak bersedih."

Al-Baqarah 2:38

So, bahagia = tiada rasa takut + tiada rasa sedih.
  • Fatir 35:3 — remember Allah's nikmat; is there any creator other than Allah who provides for you from the sky and earth? So why turn away? The answer from class: Al-Quran, and zikir pagi & petang.
  • Fatir 35:14 (with Tafsir As-Sa'di) — the idols people call on are lifeless: they don't hear your du'a, can't answer, and on the Day of Judgement they'll deny your shirk. None can inform you like the All-Knowing.

6. My own reflection — helping Allah's deen

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِن تَنصُرُوا اللَّهَ يَنصُرْكُمْ وَيُثَبِّتْ أَقْدَامَكُمْ

"Wahai orang-orang yang beriman, jika kamu menolong (agama) Allah, nescaya Allah menolong kamu dan meneguhkan kedudukan kamu."

Muhammad 47:7

Sharing Allah's ayat is one way of helping His deen — which is partly why these notes exist.

Reflections

  • Rezeki depends on Ar-Razzaq, not on what we "own". Quraysh lived in a valley with no crops, yet provision came because Allah honoured the Kaabah.
  • Nikmat can be taken back. An-Nahl 16:112 describes a town that was safe and well-provided, then denied Allah's nikmat — so Allah clothed it in hunger and fear. Ibn Kathir links this ayat to Surah Quraysh.
  • The response Allah asks for isn't just enjoying the nikmat — it's worshipping the Giver (ayat 3). Does what we have bring us closer to Allah, or make us turn away (35:3)?
