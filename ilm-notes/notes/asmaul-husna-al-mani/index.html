Al-Mani' ٱلْمَانِعُ — Yang Menghalang & Melindungi

Selasa, 6 Oktober 2026  |  Related Names: Al-Mu'ti, Al-Ghaniy, Al-Hamid

1. The Name and its meaning

Al-Mani' — the One who withholds, holds back, and protects.

From class: He holds back musibah and trials from His awliya' (those He loves), and holds back giving from whomever He wills — to test and to protect.

The root mana'a carries both meanings scholars mention: al-man'ah (protecting, defending) and al-man' (withholding — the opposite of giving).

Two forms of holding back (from class):

  1. Holding back musibah — e.g. in Surah Al-Kahf, a child was taken to prevent a greater harm: his parents were believers, and it was feared he'd drag them into rebellion and disbelief; Allah would replace him with one purer and more loving (18:74, 80–81).
  2. Holding back a gift — to test, and as a shield.
A Name or an attribute? That Allah gives and withholds is certain (Bukhari 844, Muslim 593, Fatir 35:2) — no dispute on the meaning. Whether "Al-Mani'" is one of His Names is a recognised difference: it appears in the list of 99 names in Tirmidhi 3507, which is graded da'if (many hadith scholars consider the list an addition by a narrator; the hadith "Allah has 99 names" without the list is sahih — Bukhari 2736). Al-Khattabi, al-Bayhaqi, al-Qurtubi and as-Sa'di affirm it as a Name, mentioned in a pair: Al-Mu'ti Al-Mani' (the Giver, the Withholder). Others hold only "Al-Mu'ti" is established as a Name (Bukhari 3116), and "withholding" is an attribute and action. Both are views of recognised scholars.

2. We are the needy; Allah is the Rich

يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ أَنتُمُ الْفُقَرَاءُ إِلَى اللَّهِ ۖ وَاللَّهُ هُوَ الْغَنِيُّ الْحَمِيدُ

"Wahai manusia! Kamulah yang berhajat kepada Allah, dan Allah, Dialah Yang Maha Kaya lagi Maha Terpuji."

Fatir 35:15

Al-Ghaniy = the Rich, who needs no one. Al-Hamid = the Praiseworthy — in all His actions, decrees and laws. Not everyone rich is praiseworthy; Allah is both.

3. Who are Allah's awliya'?

أَلَا إِنَّ أَوْلِيَاءَ اللَّهِ لَا خَوْفٌ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحْزَنُونَ ۝ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَكَانُوا يَتَّقُونَ

"Ketahuilah, sesungguhnya wali-wali Allah itu tiada ketakutan atas mereka dan mereka tidak bersedih hati. (Iaitu) orang-orang yang beriman dan sentiasa bertakwa."

Yunus 10:62–63

No anxiety in their hearts — calm, not grieving. Their traits: iman + taqwa — doing what Allah commands, leaving what He forbids. The ayat defines a wali by iman and taqwa; it isn't an exclusive title.

4. No one can hold back His mercy

مَّا يَفْتَحِ اللَّهُ لِلنَّاسِ مِن رَّحْمَةٍ فَلَا مُمْسِكَ لَهَا ۖ وَمَا يُمْسِكْ فَلَا مُرْسِلَ لَهُ مِن بَعْدِهِ ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَزِيزُ الْحَكِيمُ

"Apa sahaja rahmat yang Allah bukakan kepada manusia, tiada sesiapa yang dapat menahannya; dan apa yang Dia tahan, tiada sesiapa yang dapat melepaskannya selepas itu. Dan Dialah Yang Maha Perkasa lagi Maha Bijaksana."

Fatir 35:2

What Allah opens for you of His mercy — nothing can stop it. What He holds back — nothing and no one can release it.

5. Al-Mani' in our daily zikir

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، اللَّهُمَّ لَا مَانِعَ لِمَا أَعْطَيْتَ، وَلَا مُعْطِيَ لِمَا مَنَعْتَ، وَلَا يَنْفَعُ ذَا الْجَدِّ مِنْكَ الْجَدُّ

"Tiada tuhan melainkan Allah, Yang Esa, tiada sekutu bagi-Nya. Milik-Nya kerajaan dan milik-Nya segala pujian, dan Dia Maha Berkuasa atas segala sesuatu. Ya Allah, tiada yang dapat menghalang apa yang Engkau beri, tiada yang dapat memberi apa yang Engkau halang, dan tidak bermanfaat kekayaan orang yang kaya di sisi-Mu."

Bukhari 844; Muslim 593 (al-Mughirah bin Syu'bah) · Sahih

The Prophet ﷺ said this after every fardu prayer — a daily habit that ties us to the meaning of this Name.

6. Nikmat are meant to flow through us

From class: the nikmat Allah gives us are meant to be passed on to others — and holding them back can mean losing them. Doing good to others is really good for us. This meaning is firmly established:

  • "Janganlah engkau menahan (hartamu), nanti Allah menahan (rezeki-Nya) daripadamu." (Bukhari 1433, Asma' r.a.)
  • Every morning two angels descend; one says: "Ya Allah, berilah ganti kepada orang yang berinfak," and the other: "Ya Allah, berilah kebinasaan kepada orang yang menahan (hartanya)." (Bukhari 1442)
  • Saba' 34:39 — "Dan apa sahaja yang kamu belanjakan, maka Allah akan menggantikannya, dan Dialah sebaik-baik Pemberi rezeki."

7. Allah holds back dunia to give something better

From class: Allah may hold back dunia from a believer to give what's better than dunia — peace, khusyuk.

إِذَا أَحَبَّ اللَّهُ عَبْدًا حَمَاهُ الدُّنْيَا كَمَا يَظَلُّ أَحَدُكُمْ يَحْمِي سَقِيمَهُ الْمَاءَ

"Apabila Allah mencintai seorang hamba, Dia melindunginya daripada dunia, sebagaimana salah seorang daripada kamu melindungi pesakitnya daripada air."

Tirmidhi 2036 (Qatadah bin an-Nu'man) · Sahih (Darussalam); at-Tirmidhi: hasan gharib

8. Effort, and redha

  • A saying of the scholars (not a hadith): if people knew Allah's decree to its end, they'd surrender completely.
  • Effort → redha. For dunia we push ourselves to the limit; for deen, we rarely try again — one heavy moment with solat or the Quran and we let go. What we chase in dunia, even when we get it, gets left behind. What we push for in deen — that continues.

9. Relationships in the akhirah

الْأَخِلَّاءُ يَوْمَئِذٍ بَعْضُهُمْ لِبَعْضٍ عَدُوٌّ إِلَّا الْمُتَّقِينَ

"Teman-teman rapat pada hari itu menjadi musuh antara satu sama lain, kecuali orang-orang yang bertakwa."

Az-Zukhruf 43:67

On that Day everyone will claim their rights — even between parents and children: did the child fulfil their duty, and did the parent? Each person will flee from their brother, mother, father, spouse and children ('Abasa 80:34–37), and no one carries another's burden, even close family (Fatir 35:18).

But believing families are reunited, not separated: "Dan orang-orang yang beriman, dan zuriat mereka mengikuti mereka dalam keimanan, Kami pertemukan zuriat mereka dengan mereka" (At-Tur 52:21). The exception "except the muttaqin" applies to families too.

10. What knowing Al-Mani' does to us

  1. Makes us patient and content with what Allah decrees — what He gives or withholds is based on wisdom and our good.
  2. Makes us generous with what we have — whatever we give, Allah replaces; He is the Giver.
  3. Keeps us from pride and arrogance about what we own — it's from Allah, as a test.
  4. Makes us long for Allah's everlasting gifts in the akhirah, and fear being held back from the lasting nikmat more than the temporary.

Reflections

  • We're channels, not destinations. If nikmat are meant to flow through us, then holding everything tight is like blocking the pipe — and Bukhari 1433 warns what happens then.
  • "La mani'a lima a'tayta" sets our limits. No plan can guarantee what Allah withholds, or block what He gives. Our part is effort; the outcome is redha.
  • Some "no"s are protection. Like a patient kept from what would harm them (Tirmidhi 2036), a closed door may be care, not rejection. Measure it by what's given in its place: peace, khusyuk, time.
