Zikir Pagi & Petang

Morning & evening adhkar from the Sunnah  |  Arabic, maksud, how many times, and the source
In short
  • Pagi: after Subuh until sunrise. Petang: after Asar until Maghrib.
  • Most of the wording is the same for both times — only asbahna (pagi) becomes amsayna (petang). Use the switch below.
  • Can't finish all? Start with a few and keep them consistent. Small and steady beats big and stop.

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اذْكُرُوا اللَّهَ ذِكْرًا كَثِيرًا ﴿٤١﴾ وَسَبِّحُوهُ بُكْرَةً وَأَصِيلًا ﴿٤٢﴾

"Wahai orang-orang beriman, ingatlah Allah dengan zikir yang sebanyak-banyaknya, dan bertasbihlah kepada-Nya pada waktu pagi dan petang."

Al-Ahzab 33:41–42

Waktu afdhal — pagi: after Subuh until sunrise; petang: after Asar until Maghrib ("…bertasbihlah sambil memuji Tuhanmu sebelum terbit matahari dan sebelum terbenam", Qaf 50:39). Waktu yang dibolehkan if you're too busy — pagi: until the end of Dhuha (about 15 minutes before Zohor); petang: until a third of the night ("…pada waktu petang dan pagi", Ghafir 40:55).

1. From the Quran

Ayat al-Kursi

اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ ۚ لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ ۚ لَّهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ ۗ مَن ذَا الَّذِي يَشْفَعُ عِندَهُ إِلَّا بِإِذْنِهِ ۚ يَعْلَمُ مَا بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ وَمَا خَلْفَهُمْ ۖ وَلَا يُحِيطُونَ بِشَيْءٍ مِّنْ عِلْمِهِ إِلَّا بِمَا شَاءَ ۚ وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ ۖ وَلَا يَئُودُهُ حِفْظُهُمَا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْعَظِيمُ

Al-Baqarah 2:255. Whoever reads it in the morning is protected from the jinn until evening, and whoever reads it in the evening is protected until morning.

An-Nasa'i (al-Kubra); al-Hakim · Sahih (al-Albani, Sahih at-Targhib 662)

3 Qul — Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, An-Nas

قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ ﴿١﴾ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ ﴿٢﴾ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ ﴿٣﴾ وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ ﴿٤﴾

قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ ﴿١﴾ مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ ﴿٢﴾ وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ ﴿٣﴾ وَمِن شَرِّ النَّفَّاثَاتِ فِي الْعُقَدِ ﴿٤﴾ وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ ﴿٥﴾

قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ ﴿١﴾ مَلِكِ النَّاسِ ﴿٢﴾ إِلَٰهِ النَّاسِ ﴿٣﴾ مِن شَرِّ الْوَسْوَاسِ الْخَنَّاسِ ﴿٤﴾ الَّذِي يُوَسْوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ النَّاسِ ﴿٥﴾ مِنَ الْجِنَّةِ وَالنَّاسِ ﴿٦﴾

Read all three, three times each. "It will suffice you against everything."

Abu Dawud 5082; Tirmidhi 3575 · Hasan

Covered in class: Al-Ikhlas · Al-Falaq · An-Nas. The last two ayat of Al-Baqarah are a night practice (Bukhari 5009), not specifically morning/evening.

2. Doa pagi & petang

The kingdom belongs to Allah

Muslim 2723 (Ibn Mas'ud) · Sahih

By You we live, by You we die

Abu Dawud 5068; Tirmidhi 3391 · Sahih (al-Albani)

Sayyidul Istighfar

اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَىٰ عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ، وَأَبُوءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِي، فَاغْفِرْ لِي، فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ

"Ya Allah, Engkau Tuhanku, tiada tuhan selain Engkau. Engkau menciptakan aku dan aku hamba-Mu. Aku berpegang pada janji-Mu sedaya upayaku. Aku berlindung dari keburukan perbuatanku. Aku mengakui nikmat-Mu kepadaku dan mengakui dosaku, maka ampunilah aku, kerana tiada yang mengampuni dosa selain Engkau."

Whoever says it in the day with conviction and dies before evening is among the people of Jannah — and the same for the night.

Bukhari 6306 (Shaddad bin Aws) · Sahih

Health of body, hearing and sight

اللَّهُمَّ عَافِنِي فِي بَدَنِي، اللَّهُمَّ عَافِنِي فِي سَمْعِي، اللَّهُمَّ عَافِنِي فِي بَصَرِي، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ. اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْكُفْرِ وَالْفَقْرِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ

"Ya Allah, sihatkan tubuhku, pendengaranku dan penglihatanku. Tiada tuhan selain Engkau. Ya Allah, aku berlindung dari kekufuran dan kefakiran, dan dari azab kubur. Tiada tuhan selain Engkau."

Abu Dawud 5090 (Abu Bakrah) · Hasan isnad (al-Albani)

'Afwa & 'afiyah — in my deen, family and wealth

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ فِي دِينِي وَدُنْيَايَ وَأَهْلِي وَمَالِي، اللَّهُمَّ اسْتُرْ عَوْرَاتِي وَآمِنْ رَوْعَاتِي، اللَّهُمَّ احْفَظْنِي مِنْ بَيْنِ يَدَيَّ وَمِنْ خَلْفِي، وَعَنْ يَمِينِي وَعَنْ شِمَالِي، وَمِنْ فَوْقِي، وَأَعُوذُ بِعَظَمَتِكَ أَنْ أُغْتَالَ مِنْ تَحْتِي

"Ya Allah, aku memohon kemaafan dan kesejahteraan di dunia dan akhirat. Ya Allah, aku memohon kemaafan dan kesejahteraan dalam agamaku, duniaku, keluargaku dan hartaku. Ya Allah, tutuplah aibku dan tenangkan ketakutanku. Ya Allah, peliharalah aku dari depan, belakang, kanan, kiri dan atasku, dan aku berlindung dengan keagungan-Mu daripada dibinasakan dari bawahku."

Abu Dawud 5074; Ibn Majah 3871 (Ibn 'Umar) · Sahih

The Prophet ﷺ never left this one, morning or evening. Notice what's in it: ahli wa mali — our family and our wealth are things we ask Allah to keep safe, not things we keep safe ourselves.

Knower of the unseen

اللَّهُمَّ عَالِمَ الْغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ، فَاطِرَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، رَبَّ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ وَمَلِيكَهُ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ نَفْسِي، وَمِنْ شَرِّ الشَّيْطَانِ وَشِرْكِهِ، وَأَنْ أَقْتَرِفَ عَلَىٰ نَفْسِي سُوءًا أَوْ أَجُرَّهُ إِلَىٰ مُسْلِمٍ

"Ya Allah, Yang Mengetahui yang ghaib dan yang nyata, Pencipta langit dan bumi, Tuhan dan Pemilik segala sesuatu. Aku bersaksi tiada tuhan selain Engkau. Aku berlindung dari keburukan diriku, dari keburukan syaitan dan ajakan syiriknya, dan dari melakukan keburukan terhadap diriku atau menyebabkannya kepada seorang Muslim."

Taught to Abu Bakr r.a. for morning, evening and before sleep.

Abu Dawud 5067; Tirmidhi 3392 · Sahih

Bismillah — nothing can harm

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الَّذِي لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَاءِ، وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ

"Dengan nama Allah, yang bersama nama-Nya tiada sesuatu pun di bumi dan di langit dapat memberi mudarat, dan Dia Maha Mendengar lagi Maha Mengetahui." — Three times, and nothing will harm him (until evening / until morning).

Abu Dawud 5088; Tirmidhi 3388 (Uthman bin 'Affan) · Hasan sahih

Ya Hayyu Ya Qayyum

يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ بِرَحْمَتِكَ أَسْتَغِيثُ، أَصْلِحْ لِي شَأْنِي كُلَّهُ، وَلَا تَكِلْنِي إِلَىٰ نَفْسِي طَرْفَةَ عَيْنٍ

"Wahai Yang Maha Hidup, wahai Yang Maha Berdiri Sendiri, dengan rahmat-Mu aku memohon pertolongan. Perbaikilah seluruh urusanku, dan jangan serahkan aku kepada diriku walau sekelip mata." — Taught by the Prophet ﷺ to Fatimah r.a. for morning and evening.

An-Nasa'i (al-Kubra); al-Hakim (Anas) · Hasan (al-Albani, Sahih at-Targhib 661)

Upon the fitrah of Islam

"Kami berada di atas fitrah Islam, kalimah ikhlas (tauhid), agama Nabi kami Muhammad ﷺ, dan millah bapa kami Ibrahim yang lurus lagi Muslim, dan dia bukan daripada golongan musyrik."

Ahmad 15360 · Sahih (al-Albani, Sahih al-Jami' 4674)

3. Zikir with counts

Subhanallahi wa bihamdih

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ

100 times morning and evening — no one comes on the Day of Judgement with better, except someone who said the same or more.

Muslim 2692 (Abu Hurairah) · Sahih

La ilaha illallah wahdahu… (10× or 100×)

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

100 times a day: reward of freeing 10 slaves, 100 hasanah written, 100 sins erased, and a shield from syaitan for that day until evening.

Bukhari 3293; Muslim 2691 (Abu Hurairah) · Sahih

Class booklet: 10× or 100×. Said 10 times, it is like freeing four slaves from the descendants of Isma'il (Muslim 2693). Start with 10 if 100 feels heavy.

Pagi only — the heaviest tasbih

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ، عَدَدَ خَلْقِهِ، وَرِضَا نَفْسِهِ، وَزِنَةَ عَرْشِهِ، وَمِدَادَ كَلِمَاتِهِ

"Maha Suci Allah dan segala puji bagi-Nya, sebanyak bilangan makhluk-Nya, seredha diri-Nya, seberat 'Arasy-Nya, dan sebanyak tinta kalimah-Nya." — The Prophet ﷺ told Juwayriyah r.a., who had sat in zikir from Subuh till mid-morning, that these words three times outweigh all of it.

Muslim 2726 (Juwayriyah) · Sahih

Pagi only — after Subuh

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا، وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا، وَعَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا

"Ya Allah, aku memohon ilmu yang bermanfaat, rezeki yang baik, dan amalan yang diterima."

Ibn Majah 925 (Ummu Salamah) · Sahih (al-Albani)

Istighfar & taubat

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ

"O people, repent to Allah — for I repent to Him a hundred times a day."

Muslim 2702 (al-Agharr al-Muzani) · Sahih

A daily practice rather than a fixed morning/evening one — but it fits naturally in either sitting.

Petang only — the perfect words of Allah

أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ

"Aku berlindung dengan kalimah-kalimah Allah yang sempurna daripada kejahatan makhluk yang Dia ciptakan." — A man was stung by a scorpion at night; the Prophet ﷺ said had he said this in the evening, it would not have harmed him.

Muslim 2709 (Abu Hurairah) · Sahih; 3× in Tirmidhi 3604b · Sahih

Class booklet says 1×. Muslim's narration gives no count; at-Tirmidhi's says three times in the evening. Either is fine.

✓ Alhamdulillah — all done. May Allah accept it.

4. Common in booklets, but graded differently

These appear in many zikir booklets and apps. I've kept them out of the main list because of their grading, but put them here so you know where each stands. Where scholars differ, both views are noted.

ZikirSourceGrading
Allahumma inni asbahtu usy-hiduka… (4×, freed from the Fire)Abu Dawud 5069Da'if (al-Albani)
Allahumma ma asbaha bi min ni'matin…Abu Dawud 5073Da'if (al-Albani)
Radhitu billahi rabba, wa bil-islami dina, wa bi Muhammadin nabiyya (3× morning & evening) — in the class booklet (#10)Abu Dawud 5072; Ahmad; Tirmidhi 3389Differs. At-Tirmidhi called his chain hasan gharib; al-Albani graded the morning/evening version da'if (narrator Sabiq bin Najiyah is unknown). The same words with "wajabat lahul jannah", not tied to morning/evening, are sahih (Muslim 1884), and after the azan (Muslim 386). ⚠ Pending ustaz.
Hasbiyallahu la ilaha illa huwa… (7×)Abu Dawud 5081Differs. It's a saying of Abu ad-Darda' r.a., not the Prophet ﷺ. Al-Albani: mawdu' as a hadith. Ibn Baz: chain is good and it takes the ruling of a hadith. The words themselves are an ayat (At-Tawbah 9:129), so reading it as Quran is fine.
Salawat 10× morning & eveningAt-TabaraniChain discussed — graded weak by several muhaddithin. Salawat itself is always encouraged: one salawat, ten from Allah (Muslim 408).

Not saying these are wrong to read as general du'a — just that we shouldn't attach the specific fadhilat to the Prophet ﷺ unless it's established. If your class or ustaz uses one of these, it's worth asking which view they follow.

Reflections

  • Asbahna wa asbahal mulku lillah — the first thing we say each morning is that the kingdom isn't ours. Before checking the phone, the bank app, or the to-do list, we put things back in the right order.
  • We ask for 'afiyah in "ahli wa mali" every single day. It's a quiet reminder that protecting family and wealth starts with du'a, then planning — not the other way around.
  • "Wa la takilni ila nafsi tarfata 'ain" — don't leave me to myself even for a blink. The most capable person in the room still needs this.
