Kenapa perintah tidak keluar pada hari bicara?Why isn't the order issued on the hearing day?
Ada tempoh rayuan 14 hari. Mana-mana waris atau pihak berkepentingan yang tidak berpuas hati boleh merayu ke Mahkamah Tinggi melalui Pentadbir Tanah (s.29 Akta 98). Jika tiada rayuan, perintah dikeluarkan, lazimnya dalam 30 hari selepas bicara. Keputusan Mahkamah Tinggi adalah muktamad. Jika ada pertikaian, di sinilah kes berpindah ke Mahkamah Tinggi dan tempoh memanjang. There is a 14-day appeal window. Any heir or interested party who is dissatisfied can appeal to the High Court through the Land Administrator (s.29 Act 98). If no one appeals, the order is issued, usually within 30 days of the hearing. The High Court's decision is final. If there is a dispute, this is where the case moves to the High Court and the timeline stretches.
Sijil faraid dari Mahkamah Tinggi Syariah, jika diarahkan, bermula sekitar RM6,000 dan tidak ditanggung. Pusaka kecil memerlukan persetujuan semua waris; satu waris yang enggan boleh menangguhkan bicara. Pentadbiran pusaka orang Islam berjalan di saluran sivil; Mahkamah Syariah hanya terlibat bagi isu hukum seperti sijil faraid, pengesahan hibah atau wasiat, dan tuntutan harta sepencarian yang dipertikai.A faraid certificate from the Syariah High Court, if required, starts around RM6,000 and is not covered. Small-estate hearings need all heirs to agree; one objecting heir can stall the hearing. Muslim estates are administered through civil channels; the Syariah Court only steps in on questions of hukum such as faraid certificates, validating hibah or wasiat, and disputed jointly acquired property claims.
